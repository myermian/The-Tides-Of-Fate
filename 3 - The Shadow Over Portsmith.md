# Chapter 3: The Shadow Over Portsmith

## Chapter Overview & DM Notes
* **Location:** The fallen island of Portsmith and Malakor’s stronghold.
* **Atmosphere:** Gloomy, ruined, and stifling. The sky above is locked in a blood-red twilight beneath a full moon.
* **Key NPC (Optional):** **Sir Roderick the Unyielding** (located at Malek’s Old Manor). If visited, he reveals Malakor’s origin and provides a key narrative payoff during the final boss climax.
* **Orbs Mechanics:** Because the party cleansed all three shrines in Chapter 2, **Vespera, Ignis, and Null are permanently weakened** during their group fight in the castle.
* **Point of No Return:** Once the party steps through the heavy iron gates of Malakor's Castle, the gates slam shut and lock magnetically via Abyssal magic. There is no retreat until Malakor is defeated.

---

## Scene 1: Arrival at Ruined Portsmith

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> *The Salty Sea-Dog* grinds against the charred wood of a dead harbor. The air smells of ash, cold stone, and old blood. Where a thriving port should be, ruined storefronts and shattered homes sit shrouded in eerie silence.
>
> Captain Tobias steps onto the dock, scanning the skyline with a sorrowful expression. "I've traded in Portsmith a dozen times," he says softly. "It was bright, vibrant... full of laughter and song. I barely recognize it now."
>
> He reaches into a wooden chest near the helm, pulling out a set of thick glass vials filled with a glowing, dense golden liquid, and hands one to each of you alongside your standard salves.
>
> "Cora brewed these for the final push," Tobias says, his face grave. "These are Potions of Full Healing. Drinking one takes a **full action**, but it will completely restore your health to maximum, no matter how badly beaten you are. It won't clear any lingering curses or ailments, but it'll keep you breathing."
>
> He points up the winding stone path toward a cliffside estate overlooking the ruined town, then shifts his gaze further up toward the looming volcanic spire beyond. 
>
> "Before he became the Dark Lord Malakor, he was just Lord Malek. He lived up there in that large manor at the top of the hill. But when the darkness took him, he abandoned it and seized the royal castle at the foot of the mountain. Be careful out there—this town is dead, but it isn't empty."

### Scene 2A: The Manor & Sir Roderick

Inside the dusty, cobweb-covered grand foyer of Malek’s manor, the party finds a towering man in tarnished plate armor kneeling before a fireplace. This is **Sir Roderick the Unyielding**, Malek’s former companion-in-arms.

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> The warrior rises slowly, his hand resting on the pommel of a battered longsword. His face is lined with deep grief and pale with fear, though his posture betrays elite combat prowess.
>
> "More victims for the butcher's block?" Roderick asks, his voice trembling slightly. "No... you have the light of the shrines upon you."
>
> He sinks back against a dust-covered wooden table, looking up at a faded family portrait hanging above the mantel. It depicts a handsome, smiling man standing beside a gentle woman, surrounded by three young, happy children.

#### Roderick’s Lore Drop & Dialogue
* **Who Malek Was:** *"Malek was my dearest friend. A great man, brave and full of life. He was a scholar of lost history, obsessed with ocean tales and forgotten treasures."*
* **The Amulet:** *"Everything destroyed us the day he recovered an ancient amulet from a sunken wreck. The moment he put it around his neck, the curse consumed his mind. Malakor was born."*
* **The Fall of Portsmith:** *"Our king and queen were just rulers, but Malakor corrupted his own children—Ves, Ignan, and Nevin—and unleashed them upon the castle. They overthrew the crown. When the townspeople tried to flee to the ships, Malakor slayed them all in cold blood."*
* **Roderick's Shame:** *"I was a revered knight... but I was terrified. Instead of drawing my sword to protect the people, I fled. I hid here in his old home like a coward, hoping he would forget I existed."*
* **His Plea:** He drops to one knee before the party. *"Please... finish what I could not. End his corruption. Break the amulet and set his soul—and this land—free."*

> **DM Note:** Interacting with Sir Roderick flags the **[Roderick’s Redemption]** narrative branch for the chapter's ending!

## Scene 4: The Great Hall & The Siblings Triumvirate

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> You burst into the sprawling, cathedral-like throne room of the castle. High above, a massive circular skylight frames a moon, shifting into a full moon phase. 
>
> At the center of the hall, resting upon a raised stone altar, lies the unconscious, glowing form of your **Angel Goddess Mother**. Standing over her is a towering figure wrapped in dark, shadowy armor—**Dark Lord Malakor**, holding a dagger pulsing with dark void energy.
>
> Before you can step forward, the heavy iron castle doors slam shut behind you with a deafening *CRASH*, sealed tight by dark runes. 
>
> Malakor doesn't even turn around. He glances back over his shoulder, a translucent, impenetrable **Abyssal Shield** surrounding his body.
>
> *"The full moon reaches its zenith,"* Malakor’s voice echoes like grinding stone. *"Childish pests... you are too late for the blood ritual. Children! Dispose of them!"*
>
> Vespera, Ignis, and Null step forward in unison, drawing their weapons. Though their aura is visibly dimmed and weakened by the cleansed orb shrines, fighting all three together presents a formidable wall of steel, blood, and shadow.

DM NOTE: Any player attempt to attack or move toward Malakor during this phase automatically fails against his impenetrable Abyssal Shield. Focus strictly on the 3 siblings!

## Scene 5: Malakor Unbound

Upon the defeat of the three siblings, they collapse to the stone floor, their dark forms dissolving into harmless smoke. 

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> Malakor turns slowly, staring down at the dissolved remnants of his children with utter disdain. 
>
> *"Useless fools,"* Malakor sneers, stepping off the dais as his Abyssal Shield dissipates into thin air. *"Defeated by mortals. No matter. The ritual is nearly complete, and I will tear your souls apart with my own hands!"*

## !! BATTLE: Dark Lord Malakor (Final Boss)

### Boss Stat Block: Dark Lord Malakor

| Stat | Value | Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Armor Class** | 20 (Abyssal Plate) | **Hit Points** | 320 |
| **Speed** | 40 ft, Fly 30 ft | **Saving Throws** | STR +12, CON +11, WIS +8, CHA +10 |
| **Damage Resistances**| Cold, Fire, Lightning | **Damage Immunities** | Necrotic, Poison |
| **Condition Immunities**| Charmed, Frightened, Prone, Stunned | **Senses** | Truesight 120 ft |

#### Actions (DC 19 Spell Save / +12 to Hit)
* **Multiattack:** Malakor makes three attacks with his *Abyssal Greatsword* or uses *Void Obliteration* once and makes one weapon attack.
* **Abyssal Greatsword:** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 2d10 + 7 slashing damage + 2d8 necrotic damage.
* **Void Obliteration (Recharge 5–6):** Malakor fires a beam of concentrated dark energy in a 60-ft line (10 ft wide). Each creature in the line must make a **DC 19 DEX Saving Throw**, taking **10d6 force damage** on a failure, or half on a success.
* **Drain Life (1/Day):** Targets a creature within 30 ft. Target must make a **DC 19 CON Save** or take **6d10 necrotic damage**, and Malakor heals for the amount of damage dealt.

#### Legendary Actions (3 per Round)
* **1 Action - Teleport Slash:** Malakor teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space next to a target and makes one *Abyssal Greatsword* attack.
* **2 Actions - Dark Pulse:** All creatures within 15 ft must succeed on a **DC 19 STR Save** or take 3d6 necrotic damage and be pushed 20 ft away.
* **3 Actions - Shadow Channel:** Recharges *Void Obliteration* immediately.

## Scene 6: The Climax & Shattering the Amulet

When Malakor reaches 0 HP, he staggers backward toward the altar, but refuses to yield.

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> Malakor drops to one knee, coughing up dark obsidian blood. Above, the clouds part completely, and a radiant beam of moonlight shines through the high skylight, illuminating the motionless, glowing form of your Mother on the altar.
>
> *"No... I will not fail!"* Malakor roars, raising his corrupted ritual dagger for one final strike to drain her divine blood!

### The Breaking of the Amulet & Resolution

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> **CRACK!**
>
> The cursed amulet shatters into a thousand black shards. A horrifying screech fills the chamber as the dark abyssal entity trapped inside erupts out in a roaring vortex of shadows! 
>
> The dark entity coils around Malakor, consuming his body from the inside out before exploding in a blinding, warm flash of golden light. When the light clears, the shadow magic is completely gone.
>
> On the altar, your Mother’s eyes flutter open. The golden wings on her back unfold as she sits up, looking down at you with tears of joy.
>
> *"My children..."* she whispers, her voice filling the room like music.
>
> You race up the steps of the altar and throw your arms around her in a tearful embrace. The long nightmare is over.

## Scene 7: Epilogue & Returning Home

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> As you walk out of the castle together, you step under a sky clear of storm clouds. The sun rises over Portsmith for the first time in years, bathing the harbor in warm, golden light.

DM NOTE (If Roderick was visited):
Sir Roderick walks with the party down to the docks. When invited to join them on the ship, he smiles softly and shakes his head:
"No... my place is here. I hid while this town suffered; now I must help rebuild it and search for any other survivors. If a coward like me survived, surely others did too. Thank you for freeing my friend."

> You board *The Salty Sea-Dog* and set sail across calm, sparkling blue waters. Captain Tobias tips his hat with a grin as he steers you home.
>
> Days later, you step back onto the peaceful shores of your home island. The scent of sweet coastal grass and fresh dirt greets you as you walk up the dirt path to your family farm. 
>
> That evening, surrounded by warm candlelight, soft laughter, and fresh food, you sit around the wooden dinner table inside the farmhouse with your mother. Safe, whole, and together at last.
>
> **THE END**
