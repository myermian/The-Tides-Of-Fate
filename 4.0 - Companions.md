# 🔱 The Tides of Fate: Companions Guide

During Chapter 2, before disembarking **The Salty Sea-Dog** at a new island or location, the party can consult with one companion to gain a boost to their saving throws and a unique utility buff that is effective until they return back to the ship. The saving throw bonus is based on campaign difficulty:
* Easy: +3 Bonus
* Normal: +2 Bonus
* Hard: +1 Bonus

---

## 👤 Bram (Iron-Hand)

* **Class / Archetype:** Master Blacksmith (Artificer)
* **Saving Throw:** Grants a bonus to **Strength** and **Constitution** Saving Throws.
* **Tempered Gear:** Magic-tempers equipment, granting a flat bonus to both **Attack Rolls** and **Armor Class (AC)**.
  * **Easy:** +3 Attack Roll and +3 AC
  * **Normal:** +2 Attack Roll and +2 AC
  * **Hard:** +1 Attack Roll and +1 AC
* **Expiration:** The specialized sea-steel oils and temporary rune-etchings wear off under the friction of battle and time, returning gear to its baseline stats.

---

## 👤 Gideon (Scholar)

* **Class / Archetype:** Tactical Archivist (Inquisitive Rogue / Wizard)
* **Saving Throw:** Grants a bonus to **Intelligence** and **Wisdom** Saving Throws.
* **Tactical Dossier:** Briefs the party on local monster lore across all difficulties, revealing all monster stat block information and identifying hidden environmental hazards.
* **Expiration:** Gideon's research applies strictly to the local fauna and terrain of the specific island mapped; the notes are not useful for other locations.

---

## 👤 Lyra (Sea Siren)

* **Class / Archetype:** Sea-Singer (Bard)
* **Saving Throw:** Grants a bonus to **Charisma** and **Dexterity** Saving Throws.
* **Sailor's Shanty:** Performs ancient maritime shanties, granting every party member a **Bardic Inspiration** die before disembarking.
  * **Easy:** 1d10 Bardic Inspiration
  * **Normal:** 1d8 Bardic Inspiration
  * **Hard:** 1d6 Bardic Inspiration
* **Expiration:** The magical resonance and uplifting tempo of her sea shanty fade naturally from the mind when the party sleeps and rests aboard the vessel.

---

## 👤 Finnegan (Sorcerer)

* **Class / Archetype:** Tide-Shaper (Druid / Sorcerer)
* **Saving Throw:** Grants a bonus to **Intelligence** and **Constitution** Saving Throws.
* **Oceanic Ward:** Channels protective sea magic, granting resistance to elemental damage types (Fire, Cold, and Lightning).
  * **Easy:** Resistance to 3 Elements (Fire, Cold, and Lightning)
  * **Normal:** Resistance to 2 Elements (Party Choice)
  * **Hard:** Resistance to 1 Element (Party Choice)
* **Expiration:** The protective tide-veil woven around the party naturally dissipates as its magic weaves back into the surrounding sea.

---

## 👤 Finn (Scout)

* **Class / Archetype:** Island Scout (Rogue)
* **Saving Throw:** Grants a bonus to **Dexterity** and **Intelligence** Saving Throws.
* **Scouting Intel:** Provides a map of the island, completed with hidden paths and sentry blind spots, granting the party both a more detailed map of the island and a **Surprise Attack Round** on the first round of all non-boss combat encounters across all difficulties when applicable.
* **Expiration:** Mapped island, choke points, and patrol blind spots apply strictly to the specific island scouted and provide no tactical benefit at sea or on other islands.

---

## 👤 Cora (Apothecary)

* **Class / Archetype:** Master Apothecary (Cleric / Rogue)
* **Saving Throw:** Grants a bonus to **Wisdom** and **Charisma** Saving Throws.
* **Sea-Salve Stash:** Provides each party member with field salves that restore **1d10 HP** per use.
  * **Easy:** 3 Salves
  * **Normal:** 2 Salves
  * **Hard:** 1 Salve
* **Expiration:** Unpreserved organic sea-botanicals spoil and lose their potency, causing any unused salves to eventually break down.

---

## 👤 Ryan (Earth-Binder)

* **Class / Archetype:** Vanguard Shield-Bearer (Fighter)
* **Saving Throw:** Grants a bonus to **Strength** and **Constitution** Saving Throws.
* **Vanguard Fortitude:** Coordinates defensive drills before departure, granting bonus **Maximum Hit Points** to all party members until the next Long Rest.
  * **Easy:** +3d10 Max HP
  * **Normal:** +2d10 Max HP
  * **Hard:** +1d10 Max HP
* **Expiration:** The physical conditioning, extra padding, and tactical momentum naturally wear off as the party cools down and rests aboard the ship.
