# Chapter 1: The Ash of Oakwood

## 💬 Waking Up At The Salty Drift

You open your eyes to unfamiliar wooden rafters. The air smells of dried salt, sea-kelp, and faint chamomile. You are lying in a soft linen bed inside a warm, rustic room, heavy with sleep and salt water.

You are still half-buried in the covers, blinking and slow to rise, when the door clicks open. A young woman with messy copper hair, ink-stained fingers, and a leather apron loaded with dried herbs steps inside. Seeing your eyes open and your breathing steady, her face lights up with excited relief.

*"Oh! You're awake! Praise the tides, you're actually awake! I wasn't sure my poultices would hold against that ocean rot! I'm **Cora**, your local apothecary. **Tobias** found you floating in the sea and brought you here. I have some salves here to help you out. When you are feeling up for it, head downstairs. **Maeve** has made quite a meal for you."*

**Cora** hands each party member a **Sea-Salve** *(Bonus Action to use; restores 1d10 health)*. *"I have to get going, but I hope to see you around."* She smiles, turns around, and heads downstairs.

### 💬 Maeve's Introduction

You wake up and head downstairs. When you enter the tavern, you discover a hearty woman wiping down an oak table. She looks up at you and gives you a warm, welcoming smile. A plate of freshly baked bread, roasted roots, and pan-seared fish is set down before you.

*"Eat up!"* she says warmly. *"You've been through hell. I'm **Maeve**, owner of this fine establishment. The food here is fresh from my family farm. I hope you like fish; **Tobias** caught it this morning."*

> 📜 Whether the party asks her for information or attempts to leave, she informs the party that **Mariam** is expecting them at the **Town Hall**, but encourages them to look around **Oakwood** if they want.
>
> If the party asks **Maeve** additional questions, she apologizes and excuses herself, stating that she has to get the tavern ready for the day, suggesting to talk to **Jonah** for more information. She tells the party that he can be found in the **Town Square**.

---

## 🗺️ Exploring Oakwood

> 📜 Players are free to explore the town before going to the **Town Hall**.
>
> 💡 The town of Oakwood is quite large, so feel free to add additional content, but avoid introducing new characters. Instead, try to steer the party back towards exploring key areas of town.

* **The Docks**
* **The Forge**
* **The Salty Drift**
* **Maeve's Family Farm**
* **Cora’s Apothecary**
* **Town Square**
* **Library**
* **Town Hall**

### 📍 The Docks

You arrive at the docks and observe **Captain Tobias** mending some nets, conversing with another person. **Captain Tobias** looks up and laughs heartily, amazed that the party survived. *"Normally I don't venture out that far when a tempest brews, but something drew my eye to the water. I'm glad to see you are doing well. I'm **Tobias**, and this is my son **Ryan**."* **Ryan** looks up and greets the party. *"I'm glad to see you guys are okay. If no one has mentioned it yet, **Mariam** wants to talk to you. You can find her at the **Town Hall**."*

> 📜 If the party interacts with **Captain Tobias** or **Ryan** with any questions, they try to answer any questions they would be able to, but suggest that they talk to **Mariam** as she is expecting them.

### 📍 The Forge

You walk up to a stall with smoke pouring out of its chimney. A burly dwarf is inside, hard at work mending a sword. He notices you and finishes his work, plunging the red-hot steel into a bucket of water. *"Ha! Thought the deep took you. Sturdy lot, you all. Most folk would've been swallowed by the sea. **Mariam** asked me to fix your gear; it's with her if you want to pick it up."* He heads back to pick up the sword and begins sharpening it on his grinder.

> 📜 If the party interacts with **Bram** with any questions, he tries his best to answer any questions they ask. He eventually apologizes and tells them he has to get back to work. He informs them that if they want to talk shop later, they can find him at **The Salty Drift** drinking a beer in the evenings.

### 📍 The Salty Drift

You walk back into **The Salty Drift** and see **Maeve** behind the bar getting the tavern ready. *"Did you talk to **Mariam** yet? If not, just head to the **Town Hall**. You can't miss it; it's the large building around the **Town Square**."*

> 📜 If the party returns to **The Salty Drift**, **Maeve** reminds them to go talk to **Mariam** the first time. Upon any other subsequent visits, **Maeve** is assumed to be in the storage area getting the tavern ready for the night as she is not visible.

### 📍 Maeve's Family Farm

You arrive at what you assume is the family farm **Maeve** mentioned. It is a beautiful farm, with fields of vegetables and grains. There is a large fenced in clearing with various farm animals roaming around. At the back is her home and next to that is a large barn. You enjoy the beauty of the farm, but you recognize that there isn't much to do here because **Maeve** is not here right now.

> 📜 The party can look around the farm, but there isn't much to do since **Maeve** isn't here. The farmhouse and barn are locked up due to **Maeve** being at **The Salty Drift**.

### 📍 Cora's Apothecary

You walk by the apothecary shop belonging to **Cora**. The door is locked, so you peer through the window to see if she is inside. The shelves lining the walls are full of plants and concoctions, but it seems that **Cora** is not there at the moment.

> 📜 There is nothing much to do here because **Cora** is not at her shop; the shop is locked up.

### 📍 The Town Square

You walk into the bustling town square, a large open space with a beautiful tree at its center, the symbol of **Oakwood**. Nearby is a fountain and vendor stalls selling all sorts of goods are scattered throughout the square. From here you can see **The Library** and **Town Hall** and north of the town square is the resident homes.

> 📜 If the party is aware of **Jonah**, they can interact with him and ask questions.
>
> 💡 There are numerous vendors here, so the party can browse their shops and interact, but as the party does not have any money, there is nothing to purchase here.

#### 💬 Jonah's History Lesson

You walk by a man sitting on a bench with two young children next to him. He is providing a lesson about the history of **Thalassia** while they gaze up at him in wonder. This must be the man named **Jonah** whom **Maeve** mentioned. He seems a bit busy teaching the children, but he may also have valuable information. You ponder whether to head into the **Town Hall** or talk to **Jonah** first.

> If the party interacts with **Jonah**, convey that he is particularly distraught. **Jonah** suspects that something may have happened to **Celestia** due to the tempest and increased activity of dark beasts. Eventually, he advises the party that he has to finish teaching the children, but if they want to know more there are history books at **The Library** and that he will be there later in the evening to answer any questions they may have.

### 📍 The Library

You enter the town's library, a small space filled with rows of bookshelves holding all types of books. You look around, but it seems that no one is here right now.

> 📜 The party is free to browse the books, and they will come across a few books on the history of **Thalassia**, which will provide information on all sorts of topics about the region.
>
> 💡 The library contains the history of **Thalassia**, and as such, feel free to provide as little or as much information and lore as you want.

### 📍 Leaving Town (The Guard Post)

You approach the entrance to the town of **Oakwood** and see two men standing guard. As you attempt to walk past them, one of them moves to stand in front of you. *"Hold on there, travelers. There are dark beasts outside of town. If you wish to leave you must get permission from **Mariam** first. She's over at the **Town Hall** right now."* The other guard points to a large building in the **Town Square**. *"It's right there; you can't miss it."*

It seems that you will have to gain permission from **Mariam** if you plan to leave the town.

### 📍 Town Hall

You enter the courtyard at the entrance of the **Town Hall**. It is a beautiful space with meticulously manicured landscaping and marble statues. You enter the front doors and walk into the large hall. You aren't sure who **Mariam** is, but you venture a guess that she is the woman talking to a group of guards. She notices you and excuses herself from the group of guards to approach you.

---

## 💬 Mariam's Plea

**Mariam** walks towards you and introduces herself. *"Welcome, travelers. I am glad to see you breathing. I had **Bram** repair your gear; it's sitting in my office. You are welcome to rest up in **Oakwood** for as long as you need. But tell me... what were you doing so far out in the open seas, in the middle of a tempest like that?"*

You explain to **Mariam** how your mother **Elora** was kidnapped. You attempted to rescue her, giving chase, but the man who kidnapped her destroyed your ship and then threw you off his ship, leaving you to drown in the sea. You don't recall much after that, and the next thing you remember is waking up at **The Salty Drift**.

After hearing your story, **Mariam** sighs. *"That is terrible what happened to your mother. I'm sorry for that. We, too, in **Oakwood** have been having our fill of problems. It seems that the beasts have grown darker and more aggressive lately. **Jonah** insists that something must have happened to the guardian that protects these lands, an **Angelic Goddess** named **Celestia**. I'm not sure what the cause is, but what I do know is that we could use some help. I really hate to ask this of you, but from the looks of your gear, you seem to be the type of folk who are very skilled. We are able to keep these dark beasts from entering town at the entrance near the **Dark Forest**, but **Maeve** has been having issues at her family farm. Is it possible that you can help us?"*

> 📜 The branching choice to choose to help or decline to help will ultimately lead to the same battle, but with different conditions. This will demonstrate that some decisions will benefit the party, whereas other decisions have consequences, some more severe than others.

### ❓ Branching Choice 1: You Agree To Help

You agree to help **Mariam** and the town of **Oakwood**. She explains to you that the farm can be found at the edge of town. *"We aren't sure how, but we suspect some beasts from the **Dark Forest** found their way to the farm. We believe that they are hiding somewhere nearby, but our guards could not locate them."*

You pick up your gear from her office and head out to the farm. You arrive at the farm and look around, but do not find any beasts. All you can see is the beauty of the wheat fields, the vegetables, and the clearing with the animals. Suddenly, something catches your eye. You notice something running behind the barn. You head in that direction and quietly turn the corner, discovering a dark beast. It seems to be enjoying a meal, and it has not noticed you yet. You catch sight of it before it sees you — the chance to strike first is yours.

### ❓ Branching Choice 2: You Decline To Help

You decline to help **Mariam** and the town of **Oakwood**. She expresses some disappointment, but also a bit of understanding. *"I see... You have your own burdens. I hope that you find success in rescuing your mother. Your gear is in my office; feel free to pick it up. You are still welcome in **Oakwood**, but if you wish to leave, I will inform the guards you are free to do so."* She excuses herself and heads back to a group of guards to discuss what to do next regarding the dark beasts.

You pick up your gear from her office and exit the **Town Hall**, walking into the **Town Square**. You feel a bit of guilt about declining to help. Before you can gather your thoughts, you hear screams and see townsfolk running towards the **Town Hall**. A dark beast enters the **Town Square**, thrashing about. It wrecks various vendor stalls before it turns its sights on you. Locking onto you, it charges in your direction and lunges at you.

### ⚔️ First Encounter With A Dark Beast

🐉 [Gloomgura]

> 📜 Agreeing to help or declining to help **Mariam** and the town of **Oakwood** affects this battle. This is a story-required battle, and as such, there is no option to escape.
>
> Agreeing to help will result in fighting the dark beast at **Maeve's Family Farm**, and gives the opportunity for the party to have an **Advantage** on the first round attack. Afterwards, the party heads back towards the **Town Hall** to give **Mariam** an update on what transpired, but they run into her at the **Town Square**.
>
> Declining to help will result in fighting the dark beast in the **Town Square**, and give the party a **Disadvantage** on the first round attack. Afterwards, **Mariam** exits the **Town Hall** to observe the situation.

Upon defeating the **Gloomgura**, it cries out in agony before dissolving into a mist of dark energy and fading into nothingness. The **Gloomgura** is gone, but the damage it has caused to **Oakwood** remains.

---

## 💬 The Kidnapping

**Mariam** approaches the group in the **Town Square**, but before she can utter a word, **Jonah** runs toward **Mariam**, screaming, *"**Mariam**, the children have been taken! **Lily** snuck outside the front gates to pick some wildflowers. **Kaelen** saw a **Corrupted Mire-Troll** approaching his sister, so he ran to get her. Before anyone could react, it grabbed both children and dragged them into the **Dark Forest**. I told **Tobias** and **Ryan** what happened, and they gave chase, but they have not returned. I fear the dark beasts are too strong for them."*

**Mariam** is aghast at hearing what has happened. *"Oh my heavens, that is terrible news."* She looks to you and begs for your assistance. *"Those children are **Oakwood**, and **Tobias** and **Ryan** do not stand a chance in there! This town needs you; please, I beg you, help us!"*

### ❓ Branching Choice 1: You Agree To Help

You agree to help rescue the children. **Mariam** expresses relief and informs the guards to let you pass. You rush out and head towards the **Dark Forest** with **Jonah** and some town guards right behind you.

### ❓ Branching Choice 2: You Decline To Help

You decline to help. **Mariam** and **Jonah** both express disappointment. *"I guess it will be up to **Oakwood** to handle its own affairs then."* They gather some town guards and head towards the forest.

They return to **Oakwood** carrying a severely injured **Captain Tobias** and **Ryan**, but without the children in tow. The townsfolk of **Oakwood** look at you with daggers in their eyes, murmuring about your cowardice. One man approaches you. *"This town helped you when you needed it most, and even though you have the skills to help us, you turn your backs on us. You aren't welcome here anymore; you should just leave."*

You look around and decide it is better to leave than to stand in the center of a mob, so you head toward the front gate. Suddenly, a horde of dark beasts breaks past the guards standing post, easily disposing of them. Hundreds of beasts begin to pour into town. The situation is too overwhelming for the few remaining guards. **Cora** and **Maeve** rush into the **Town Square** because of the commotion, only to fall victim as well. The pack of dark beasts surrounds you, and although you could stand your ground against a smaller group, the sheer number of beasts is too much for you to handle. You perish along with the whole of **Oakwood**.

> 📜 The party declining to help has severe consequences; it is essentially the party giving up on the campaign itself. As such, the campaign comes to an end.
>
> 💡 If the consequence of ending the campaign with a total party kill is too severe, feel free to modify the content to push the party in the right direction.

### 💬 The Forest Entrance

You reach the treeline where they discover **Captain Tobias** and **Ryan** half buried in the moss at the mouth of the forest, lying battered and incapacitated. Their armor is scored and dented, their cloaks torn. The dark beasts of the forest attacked them. In their desperate escape, the twisting paths folded them right back out to the forest's entrance. **Ryan** coughs weakly, pointing past the trees. *"It... took them deeper... the paths... never stopped bending back on themselves..."*

**Jonah** arrives with a few of the guards to discover **Captain Tobias** and **Ryan** lying on the ground. The guards begin carrying the two back to safety. **Jonah** grabs your arm and looks directly at you. *"Please, hear me out before anyone goes in there."* **Jonah** points a trembling finger toward the drag marks leading into the forest. *"The **Dark Forest** isn't any ordinary forest. The darkness that has overtaken these woods runs thick and black, and the trees rearrange as you walk. It plays tricks on folk. Just the other day, a guard chased after a dark beast into the forest. No matter which path they swore they took, they always stumbled back out at the same entrance, unsure how long they'd been inside. You'll have to be steady, and you'll have to be sure. If you let your attention wander, the forest will return you back to where you started or worse yet, never at all."* He turns around and heads back into town.

You are left standing at the entrance, observing drag marks leading into the **Dark Forest** with the children somewhere deep inside.

### 💬 The Dark Forest

You head into the **Dark Forest**, and the trees close behind you almost immediately. A thick, black fog swallows the trail, and the light dims to a pale green. The air is still and damp, and somewhere close, a stream is running, though you can't see it. A stain of blood marks the soft moss before you. You make your way farther in, following the path until you reach a juncture. You are unsure of which path to take, as there are three laid out before you: left, right, or straight ahead. It seems that the forest is alive, and it is paying attention.

> 📜 The **Dark Forest** is a maze composed of five areas: The forest entrance at the edge of town, four crossroads, the forest lake (safe area), an additional four crossroads, and the forest clearing. The eight crossroads always present the party with three choices: left, right or straight ahead.
>
> Select what the correct route should be and note it down. This route should not change, allowing the party to discover the correct path through trial and error or sheer luck.
>
> If the party chooses the wrong direction at any crossroads, then they will find themselves back at the forest entrance and must start over.
>
> The forest lake offers a safe area, however the party cannot take a rest due to the urgency of the situation. If they attempt to do so, they lose the safety and are interrupted by a random encounter.
>
> When the party eventually reaches the forest clearing by navigating through the crossroads correctly, they find themselves in the small clearing.
>
> 💡 Use your best judgement on the frequency of random encounters that the party runs into without overwhelming them in this section. One possibility is that the party encounters a random battle when they select the incorrect path prior to returning to the entrance.

#### 💬 Choosing The Correct Direction

You make a decision and continue forward. You head down the path and realize that this was the correct decision.

#### 💬 Choosing The Wrong Direction

You make a decision and continue forward. As soon as you start heading down this path, the already-thick black fog seems to thicken further. The path simultaneously stretches and tightens, with the trees sliding past each other like slow, breathing giants. You stumble your way around and emerge from the fog. You feel a sense of déjà vu, and for good reason. You seem to have walked back to the entrance of the forest, where you can clearly see the same drag marks leading into the forest. It is as if none of what you did had ever happened. But, you are determined to rescue the children, so you head right back into the forest with the trees closing behind you once again. This area definitely feels familiar, and as such, you try very hard to remember which decisions you made before to avoid taking the wrong path again.

#### 💬 The Forest Lake (Safe Area)

You are walking along the path when you see a light shining ahead. The path seems to have led you toward a lake hidden within the forest. The dark fog has thinned a bit in this area, allowing you to take in the beauty of the lake. It is a calm, moonlit lake ringed by smooth stones and glowing white flowers. It amazes you that even with darkness overhanging this forest, its beauty can still be seen. You follow the path around the lakeshore and come across another set of drag marks leading into the farthest parts of the forest.

#### ⚔️ Random Battles (Dark Forest)

* 🐉 [Shadow Hound] (A), 🐉 [Shadow Hound] (B), 🐉 [Shadow Hound] (C)
* 🐉 [Corrupted Abomination]
* 👹 [Abyssal Gloom-Beast]

> 📜 There is a chance that the party could encounter the **Abyssal Gloom-Beast**. On the first encounter, it runs away after sustaining at least 50% HP damage. A second random encounter with the **Abyssal Gloom-Beast** resumes where the previous encounter left off. Once defeated, this dark beast cannot be encountered again.

### 💬 The Forest Clearing

You find yourself in a small clearing. The dark fog that overhangs the forest is thinner here, much like at the forest lake. Looking around, you discover that this area is clearly where the **Corrupted Mire-Troll** has made its home. You look up and discover the children suspended in a cage hanging from the thick branch of a large tree. Before you have time to think about how to rescue them, a large boulder flies past you and crashes into the ground with a thunderous boom. The ground shakes a bit, throwing you slightly off balance. The **Corrupted Mire-Troll** emerges from the trees and heads directly toward you, intent on smashing you.

#### ⚔️ A Large Encounter

👹 [Corrupted Mire-Troll]

> 📜 This is a story-required battle and as such, there is no option to escape.

With the **Corrupted Mire-Troll** having been defeated, the darkness that infected the forest seems to clear out. The black fog that covered the forest is lifted and the forest's twisting, looping paths untangle into a single, honest trail. The way back is suddenly clear and easy to follow.

---

## 💬 Oakwood Saved

You free **Kaelen** and **Lily** from the cage and guide them safely back along the now-clear trail. You emerge from the forest treeline with the children and make your way back into **Oakwood**.

### 💬 The History Of Thalassia

You enter the **Town Square** where the townsfolk around celebrate in jubilation. **Mariam** approaches you and thanks you for rescuing the children, *"Thank you so much! You have our deepest gratitude."*

**Jonah** climbs onto the bench, calling for silence. The whole of **Oakwood** comes to a hush. He speaks as he always does, with the weight of history. *"I want to thank you for helping this town. But... the darkness that descended onto us is no coincidence."* Pointing to the sky, **Jonah** recounts the historical events involving the **Angelic Goddess** and the **Dark Void Creature**. *"Thousands of years ago, an **Angelic Goddess** named **Celestia** prevented a **Dark Void Creature** from opening a portal in the moon and spreading darkness over the entire world. She sent the **Dark Void Creature** into **The Abyss**, but not before it infected her amulet. She was forced to discard the amulet to avoid the darkness from consuming her and chose to hide it in the depths of the ocean where no one would find it. Three shrines were constructed on different islands and blessed by **Celestia** in order to protect this world with **Celestial Blessings**."*

*"Between the tempest and the increased frequency of dark beasts attacking, I believe that the amulet has been discovered... by whom, I could not say. The island shrines no longer shine, so I believe the darkness within the amulet has been used to corrupt the celestial orbs at the heart of the shrines. There isn't much time. Whoever has taken this amulet has clearly been corrupted by it, and the darkness will attempt to use this person as a vehicle to open the portal by performing a **Blood Red Moon** ritual during the next full moon. If this happens, the entire world will fall into eternal darkness."*

**Jonah** looks you in the eyes. *"You have the strength, and the sea is the only road to saving these lands. Cleanse the shrines of the darkness, and its hold on the world will weaken. Then, and only then, will you have any chance of stopping whoever has the amulet."*

### 💬 Oakwood Joins The Fight

The townsfolk begin to murmur among themselves, and then complete silence falls. **Captain Tobias** steps forward, quiet and grave. *"I just want to say something. My family and I have spent our lives on these waters, and I will not let this town sink into the darkness."* He turns and looks at you. *"**The Salty Sea-Dog** is yours. Provisions for the voyage and passage across the open sea. But I'll be honest with you. Without a full crew manning the ship, we cannot hope to venture far enough with that tempest out there."* **Ryan** is the first to step forward. *"I will join you, father."* **Cora** raises her hand. *"You'll need someone who can keep everyone healthy."* Slowly, others chime in as well. **Captain Tobias** looks at the party. *"Well, it looks like we have the whole of **Oakwood** to help you out. You heard **Jonah**; there's no time to waste, so grab your gear because we head out now."*
