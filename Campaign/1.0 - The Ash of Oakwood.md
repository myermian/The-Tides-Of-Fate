# Chapter 1: The Ash of Oakwood

## 💬 Waking Up at The Salty Drift

You open your eyes to unfamiliar wooden rafters. The air smells of dried salt, sea-kelp, and faint chamomile. You are lying in a soft linen bed inside a warm, rustic room, heavy with sleep and salt water.

You are still half-buried in the covers, blinking and slow to rise, when the door clicks open. A young woman with messy copper hair, ink-stained fingers, and a leather apron loaded with dried herbs steps inside. Seeing your eyes open and your breathing steady, her face lights up with excited relief.

*"Oh! You're awake! Praise the tides, you're actually awake! I wasn't sure my poultices would hold against that ocean rot!"*

### Roleplaying Cora (Apothecary)

* **Cora's Explanation:** She introduces herself as the local apothecary. When asked where they are, she explains that **Captain Tobias** dragged them out of the ocean while riding out a storm on his fishing boat. He brought them straight to **The Salty Drift** (the village inn) so she could tend to their injuries.
* **The Salves:** She hands each player a pouch containing a **Sea-Salve** (*Bonus Action to use; restores health*). She beams with pride, telling them to keep them handy before heading out to check on other townspeople.

---

## 💬 The Main Hall & Lay of the Land

*When the players head downstairs into the inn's common room, they are greeted by **Maeve**, the innkeeper.*

Downstairs, a hearty woman wiping down an oak table gives you a warm, welcoming smile. A plate of freshly baked bread, roasted roots, and pan-seared fish is set down before you.

*"Eat up!"* she says warmly. *"You've been through hell. I'm Maeve, owner of this fine establishment."*

### Roleplaying Maeve

* **Local Info:** Maeve explains she grows most of her ingredients on her family farm on the outskirts of town.
* **Directions:** Whether the party asks or attempts to leave, she tells the party that **Mariam** is expecting them at the **Town Hall**, but encourages them to look around **Oakwood** first.
* If asked further questions, Maeve excuses herself, noting she has to get back to prepping the evening stew, and suggests talking to **Old Man Jonah** if they want local history.

---

## 💬 Exploring Oakwood

*Players are free to explore the village before going to the **Town Hall**.*

* **The Docks:** Captain Tobias and his son **Ryan** are at the docks, tending to **The Salty Sea-Dog**.
* **The Blacksmith Forge:** **Bram Iron-Hand** is at his forge, working on upgrading some weapons.
* **The Salty Drift:** Maeve is currently inside cleaning up. You can enter again, but she may be too busy to talk.
* **Cora’s Apothecary:** (*Currently empty; Cora is out gathering herbs.*)
* **The Library / Square:** Old Man Jonah is outside of the library with **Kaelen** and **Lily**.

### A. The Docks (Captain Tobias & Ryan)

* **Interaction:** Tobias is mending nets with his son, **Ryan**. 
* **Dialogue:** Tobias laughs heartily, amazed they survived: *"Normally I don't venture out that far when a tempest brews, but something pulled my eye to the water. Glad you're among the living!"*
* **Ryan:** Introduced as a quiet, heavily armored defender of the village.
* **Push:** Tobias insists they go see **Mariam** right away.

### B. The Blacksmith Forge (Bram Iron-Hand)

* **Interaction:** **Bram** is hammering iron over an anvil.
* **Dialogue:** **Bram** stops hammering, wipes his brow, and grins. *"Ha! Thought the deep took you. Sturdy lot, you all. Most folk would've been swallowed by the ocean."* He tells the party he personally repaired their weapons and armor after the shipwreck.
* **Location of Gear:** He notes that he delivered their gear straight to the **Town Hall**, so they'll need to see **Mariam** to collect it.

### C. The Town Square / Library (Jonah, Kaelen, & Lily)

* **Interaction:** Old Man Jonah is sitting on a bench teaching **Kaelen** and **Lily** how to tie naval knots.
* **Children:** **Kaelen** boasts about wanting to be a grand fisherman like his grandfather Tobias; **Lily** happily shows off a handful of wildflowers.
* **Lore Drop (Celestia):** If asked about the realm, Jonah sighs: *"It wasn't always dark skies and monsters. These isles were protected by an Angelic Goddess named **Celestia**. I fear something may have happened to her."*

### D. The Salty Drift (Maeve)

* **Interaction:** Maeve is behind the bar cleaning things up.
* **Dialogue:** Maeve behaves similarly to before, either talking about her farm, or insisting the party head to town.

### E. Cora's Apothecary (Empty)

* **Interaction:** Upon arriving, the party can see that no one is inside her shop and the door is locked.

### F. Leaving Town (The Guard Post)

* If players try to leave **Oakwood**, town guards politely block the road: *"Hold on there, travelers. The Dark Forest and the roads beyond are crawling with aggressive beasts lately.**Mariam** is waiting for you at the **Town Hall**. Speak with her first."*

## 💬 Mariam's Plea

Inside the **Town Hall**, **Mariam** sits behind a long wooden table, flanked by the party's cleaned armor and weapons.

Mariam looks up with stern, tired eyes. *"Welcome, travelers. I am glad to see you breathing. **Bram** has restored your equipment, and you are welcome to rest in **Oakwood** for as long as you need. But tell me... what were you doing so far out in the open ocean, in the middle of a tempest like that?"*

### The Conversation

- **The Party’s Story:** The players explain their quest to rescue their mother, **Elora**, from **Lord Malekor**.
- **Mariam’s Request:** Hearing of their martial prowess, Mariam’s eyes brighten with desperate hope. She explains that **Oakwood** has been plagued by an influx of corrupted sea-monsters. Specifically, Maeve’s farm on the outskirts is currently under siege. She asks if they would lend their strength to help defend the village.

### Branching Choice 1: The Party Agrees to Help
* Mariam thanks them profusely and points them toward Maeve's farm.
* **Battle Location:** Maeve's Farm.
* **Narrative Consequence:** The townsfolk have a more positive attitude towards the party.

### Branching Choice 2: The Party Declines to Help
* Mariam expresses disappointment, but understanding: *"I see. You have your own burdens. We shall handle our own troubles as best we can."*
* **Battle Location:** Oakwood Town Square.
* **Narrative Consequence:** The monsters break through the farm undefended and head straight into the village square. The townsfolk have a negative attitude towards the party.

## 💬 The Kidnapping

Immediately after the raid is repelled, **Jonah** sprints into view, out of breath and terrified. *"The children! A giant Corrupted Mire-Troll burst from the treeline during the chaos and grabbed **Kaelen** and **Lily** — it dragged them deep into the Dark Forest! **Captain Tobias** and **Ryan** went after them and they haven't come back!"*

**Mariam** steps forward and begs the party to assist. *"Those children are **Oakwood**'s own, and **Captain Tobias** and his son are lost in there! This village needs you — please, I beg you, reconsider!"*

### Branching Choice 1: The Party Agrees to Help

* The party heads straight into the Dark Forest to find the children — and their rescuers.

### Branching Choice 2: The Party Declines to Help

* The party declines and decides to stay at the inn. That night, a massive horde of monsters emerges from the Dark Forest, completely overwhelming **Oakwood**. Without the children saved or the woods cleared, the village falls, and the party is killed in the collapse. **Campaign Ends.**

### 💬 The Forest Entrance

The party reaches the treeline, and there — half buried in the moss at the very mouth of the forest — they find **Captain Tobias** and **Ryan** both lying battered and incapacitated. Their armor is scored and dented, their cloaks torn: the troll attacked them on the way in, and in their desperate escape the twisting paths folded them right back out to the forest's entrance. **Ryan** coughs weakly, pointing past the trees. *"It... took them deeper... the paths... never stopped bending back on themselves..."*

**Jonah** arrives with a few of the town guards to discover **Tobias** and **Ryan**.

*"Please, hear me out before anyone goes in."* **Jonah** points a trembling finger toward the treeline. *"The Dark Forest isn't like the woods beyond it. The fog there runs thick and black, and the trees rearrange as you walk. It plays tricks on folk — I've lost two trappers to it over the years. No matter which path they swore they took, they always stumbled back out at the same entrance, none of them sure how long they'd been walking. You'll have to be steady, and you'll have to be sure. If you let your attention wander, the forest will give you back to the door."*

**Jonah** and the guards lift **Tobias** and **Ryan** and carry them back into town to recover. The party is left standing at the forest's mouth, the children still lost beyond the treeline.

### 💬 The Dark Forest

The trees close behind you as you cross the treeline. A thick, grey fog swallows the trail, and the light dims to a pale green. The air is still and damp, and somewhere close, a stream is running — though you can't see it. On the soft moss before you, a trail of deep **drag marks** stretches ahead — the children, dragged straight onward. **The forest is alive, and it is paying attention.**

> 📜 The Dark Forest is a maze composed of five sections: The forest entrance at the edge of town, four crossroads, the forest lake (safe area), an additional four crossroads, and the forest clearing. The eight crossroads always present the party with three choices: left, right or straight ahead. If the party chooses the wrong path, they find themselves back at the entrance, described as the party recognizing the same drag marks leading into the forest.
>
> 💡 A suggested correct route is Forest Entrance, Straight, Left, Left, Right, Forest Lake, Right, Right, Right, Straight, Forest Clearing. Feel free to change the correct route to add variation.
>
> 💡 The party will run into random encounters while navigating the forest. Use your best judgement to have the party run into random encounters without overwhelming the party.
>
> **Wrong Choice:** The fog thickens until it is almost black. The trail you walked seems to stretch and tighten, the trees sliding past one another like slow, breathing giants. When the mist finally thins, you stand exactly where you began, at the forest's entrance, the same drag marks stretching straight ahead of you. It is as if none of it ever happened.
>
> Although the forest lake is a safe area, the party cannot take a rest due to the urgency of the situation.
>
> **Forest Lake (First Visit):** The fog thins a bit into a calm, moonlit lake ringed by smooth stones and glowing white flowers. You follow the path around the lake and find additional drag marks leading back into the forest.
>
> **The Forest Clearing:** Choosing the correct route through the forest carries the party out of the maze. The party finds themselves in a small clearing that is where the Mire-Troll has made his home.

## 💬 Oakwood Saved

With the Mire-Troll slain and the fog lifted, the heroes free **Kaelen** and **Lily** from the cage and guide them safely back along the now-clear trail. As you emerge from the forest treeline with the children, the entire village of **Oakwood** erupts into tears and cheers. 

### 💬 The History of Thalassia

Old Man Jonah climbs to the center of the square, his staff tapping for silence. The whole of **Oakwood** gathers. He speaks as he always does at the library, with the weight of history. Jonah recounts the old stories of **Thalassia**.

*"I want to thank you for helping this town. The darkness that descended onto us is no coincidence."* Pointing to the sky, Jonah tells the historical events involving the Angelic Goddess and the **Dark Void Creature**. *"Thousands of years ago, an Angelic Goddess named **Celestia** prevented a **Dark Void Creature** from opening a portal in the moon and spreading darkness over the entire world. She sent the **Dark Void Creature** into the abyss, but not before it infected her amulet. She was forced to discard the amulet to avoid the darkness from consuming her and chose to place it in the depths of the ocean where no one would stumble across it. Three shrines were constructed on different islands and blessed by **Celestia** in order to create a shield of protection over this world."*

*"Between the tempest and the increased dark monsters attacking, I believe that the amulet has been discovered by **Malekor**. The island shrines no longer shine, so I believe the darkness within the amulet has corrupted the mystical orbs at the heart of the shrines. There isn't much time, upon the next full moon **Malekor** can perform the **Blood Red Moon** ritual and open the dark void portal."*

Jonah looks the party in the eyes: *"You have the strength, and the sea is the only road to saving these lands. Cleanse the shrines and his power will weaken. Then, and only then, will you have any chance of reaching him."*

### 💬 Oakwood Joins The Fight

A hush falls over the square, and **Captain Tobias** steps forward, quiet and grave. *"My boys and I have spent our lives on these waters, and I will not let this village sink in the dark. **The Salty Sea-Dog** is yours. Provisions for the voyage, and passage across the open sea. But, I'll be honest with you. Without a full crew manning the ship we cannot hope to venture far enough with that tempest out there."* **Ryan** is the first to step forward. *"I will join you, Father."* **Cora** raises her hand. *"You'll need someone who can keep everyone healthy."* Slowly, others chime in as well. **Captain Tobias** looks at the party, *"Well, it looks like we have the whole of **Oakwood** to help you out. You heard **Jonah**, there's no time to waste so grab your gear because we head out in 15 minutes."*
