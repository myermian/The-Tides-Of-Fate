# Chapter 3: The Shadow Over Portsmith

## 💠 Chapter Overview

> 📜 **Crimsonspire Island** is the fallen island which holds the ruined port of **Portsmith** and the stronghold of **Malakor**. The sky above is locked in a blood-red twilight beneath a full moon, and the whole island is shrouded in gloom, ruin, and a stifling silence.
>
> The voyage to **Crimsonspire Island** is not the peaceful crossing the party expected — **Malakor** sends a **Kraken** to stop them before they reach shore. The companions, fully committed to keeping *The Salty Sea-Dog* afloat through the tempest and the battle, never get the chance to confer their usual pre-departure bonus to the party.
>
> Once the party arrives, there is no turning back; this is a point of no return until **Malakor** is defeated.

---

## 💬 The Voyage To Portsmith

*The Salty Sea-Dog* cuts through calm, open water as the island's outline grows on the horizon. But as the island draws near, the sky darkens without warning. The sun vanishes beneath a churning wall of black cloud that rolls in from the east, and the sea beneath the keel churns and foams in long, restless lines. The air grows cold, heavy, and charged, crackling with a low, constant hiss.

You have been asleep below deck during the crossing. The storm wakes you. *The Salty Sea-Dog* lurches with a bone-jarring slam, and the whole ship comes alive with shouting, the creak of straining wood, and the slap of water over the rail.

Above, **Captain Tobias** fights the helm against the rising swells. *"That ain't natural. Something's out there... and it ain't happy about us coming."* Your companions are already scattered across the deck, hands full, doing whatever they can to keep the ship afloat. It is all hands on deck, no one to spare with a special assignment. Then the sea parts.

Eight colossal tentacles, each the size of a ship's mast, rise from the black water and coil around the hull. The deck tilts violently. Water slams over the rail in great sheets. A voice shouts over the howling wind: *"**Kraken**! It's trying to destroy our ship!"*

> 📜 The party is still aboard **The Salty Sea-Dog**. The companions stay on deck, fully occupied with keeping the ship afloat and steering through the tempest. They cannot assist the party in combat during this battle.

### ⚔️ The Beast From Below

👹 [Kraken]

> 📜 The party is only required to defeat the **Kraken**. The tempest itself is an environmental hazard.
>
> 🌴 The tempest rages over the ship for the duration of the battle. At the start of each round, a bolt of dark lightning strikes a random party member, dealing **3d6 Lightning Damage**. This damage cannot be avoided or redirected — it is an environmental effect, not an attack. The lightning cannot target the **Kraken**, the companions, or the ship itself.
>
> 💡 If a party member is knocked unconscious, the companions on deck can pull them out of the way, keeping them from falling overboard. An unconscious party member is not a valid target for the lightning damage from the tempest while being held.

### 💬 The Aftermath

As the final tentacle of the **Kraken** falls, the great beast sinks beneath the black water with a long, gurgling groan. Then, without warning, a blinding bolt of lightning tears down from the tempest and strikes **The Salty Sea-Dog** dead center. The hull splits open with a sound like a thunderclap. Water pours in through the crack, rushing down into the lower deck in a freezing torrent. Your companions are on it in an instant, bracing planks and hauling loose cargo to shore up the breach. The ship is taking on water, but not enough to sink her just yet.

**Captain Tobias** grips the wheel hard, fighting the listing deck. *"Hold together, you stubborn old thing. Just a little further."*

Suddenly, the **Celestial Orbs** on the shrine islands erupt with radiant light, sending a beam of light directly into the sky. A white light descends down from the heavens in a single, blinding column, striking the heart of the tempest. The black clouds dissolve and the wind dies. The sea calms down allowing **Captain Tobias** to regain control. He guides **The Salty Sea-Dog** toward the ruined harbor of **Portsmith**, her hull cracked and her decks soaked, but still afloat. Your companions haul you aboard from the splintered deck, exhausted and soaked, their faces set with the strain of what they have just survived.

> 📜 Because the voyage was derailed by the **Kraken** battle, the companions never had the chance to confer their pre-departure companion bonus to the party. Their focus was entirely on keeping **The Salty Sea-Dog** afloat and alive through the tempest and the fight. The party arrives at **Portsmith** without any active companion bonus in effect.

## 💬 Arrival

**The Salty Sea-Dog** grinds against the charred wood of a dead harbor, her cracked hull groaning against the rotted pilings. The air smells of ash, cold stone, and old blood. Where a thriving port should be, ruined storefronts and shattered homes sit shrouded in eerie silence.

**Captain Tobias** steps onto the dock, scanning the skyline with a sorrowful expression. *"I've traded in **Portsmith** a dozen times. It was bright, vibrant... full of laughter and song. I barely recognize it now."* His gaze drifts over the blackened, gutted buildings and his jaw tightens. The gloom clings to every street, and it is plain to see that the darkness has fallen hard upon this town.

With the ship's hull still split and the water still creeping in, **Captain Tobias** turns to the makeshift crew. *"We have some work to do to repair this ship."*

**Cora** steps forward, pressing a leather pouch of vials into your hands. She meets your eyes, her usual quick grin soft with worry. *"I've been working on these since we set sail, brewing the tides and herbs into something stronger than anything I've made before. These are **Full-Healing Salves**. These will heal you completely."* You take the salve and drink it, providing you with the revitalization you needed after the battle with the **Kraken**.

**Captain Tobias** points up the winding stone path toward the looming castle crowning the hill, his finger steady despite his weariness. *"**Portsmith** had a royal family—kings and queens who looked over this town from that seat up top. Whatever darkness has swallowed this place, I'd wager its source sits in that castle. That's where you're most likely to find the one who brought all of this down on us."*

A shriek startles the group and draws their attention to the sky. You look up and see a **Dark Dragon** flying over the **Royal Palace**. **Finn** crouches by the dock's edge, one hand resting on a dagger, his eyes already reading the broken streets beyond. *"Tip of advice... I'd avoid a direct approach. I can't imagine it's any fun fighting a dragon of that size,"* he says to you. *"Search the town first. See what survived, what's still moving, where the eyes are. That's what I'd do. Going in blind is how good people get killed."*

> 📜 **Captain Tobias** and your companions stay behind to repair **The Salty Sea-Dog** and do not accompany the party into **Portsmith**. The party is free to continue onward.
>
> **Full-Healing Salves:** *(Bonus Action to use):* Using a **Full-Healing Salve** grants its wielder the full benefits of a **Long Rest**. This item is automatically consumed as part of the narrative.

### 📍 The Harbor

You return to the **Harbor**, where **The Salty Sea-Dog** rests against the rotting pilings. **Captain Tobias** and your companions are still at work on her cracked hull, hauling timber and bracing the seams. The charred docks stretch around you, and the water beyond the harbor mouth is black and still under the blood-red moon.

> 📜 **Captain Tobias** and the companions stay behind with the ship and do not accompany the party into town. Their interactions will be limited due to the fact that their focus remains on getting **The Salty Sea-Dog** seaworthy again.

### 📍 The Market

You step into the **Market**, a broad paved strip that once teemed with vendor stalls. Now the stalls are broken and gutted, their awnings torn and strewn across the cobblestones. Crates of spoiled produce and shattered bottles litter the ground, and the air carries the sweet rot of things left too long in the heat.

### 📍 The Town Square

You walk into the **Town Square**, a wide open space whose central fountain stands dry and cracked. What would once have been laughter and haggling is gone; in its place is a silence so complete that your footsteps sound like blows. The benches and paving stones are undisturbed, as though the people simply ceased to be.

### 📍 The Church of Celestia

You enter the **Church of Celestia**, a modest stone building whose pews lie overturned and whose windows hang open to the red moon. The altar stands defaced and a thin trail of dark residue runs from the altar steps toward the door.

Dominating the far wall above the altar is a vast, faded mural of **Celestia** rendered in her true form — not the mortal face she wears among the people, but a radiant figure wreathed in golden light, arms outstretched as if drawing the light of the sea into herself. Her silver-flecked eyes and golden-tinged hair still glow faintly in the gloom, and the longer you look, the more plainly you see that her face bears a stark resemblance to your own mother, **Elora**.

Set into the wall behind the altar is a heavy wooden door. It is the only door in the church, and it leads down.

#### 📍 The Church Basement

You open the door and head down the narrow set of rough-hewn stone steps into a cellar cut from the living bedrock beneath the church. The air is cold and damp, but the chamber is not empty.

Crouched in the gloom are a dozen of townsfolk of **Portsmith** — men, women, and children huddled close together, their faces gaunt with hunger and terror. They do not rise when you enter, and their eyes find you, then dart to one another, as though they are deciding whether you are a friend, a threat, or the darkness itself.

Among the huddled townsfolk sits a towering man in tarnished plate armor, his battered longsword resting across his knees.

He rises slowly, joints creaking in the still air, and studies you a long moment. Something in your eyes tells him you have not been hollowed. His voice is a low rasp unaccustomed to the quiet. *"You still walk. You still think. The darkness hasn't taken you. That's more than I can say for most of them."*

*"My name is **Sir Roderick**."* He glances back at the wide-eyed faces behind him. *"Forgive them. They've had a long time down here in the dark, and little reason left to trust."*

He turns his gaze back to you. *"How did you find your way here? Does the darkness still hang over **Portsmith**, or has it loosened its grip on the town?"*

> 📜 Whether the party asks him questions, asks the right ones, or turns to leave, **Sir Roderick** will stop them and tell them his tale. He has been holding this truth alone for too long, and he needs it heard.

#### 💬 Sir Roderick's Tale

*"It's my fault."* **Sir Roderick** exclaims. *"The man who brought this darkness was my friend, a scholar named **Malek** from **Tidefall**. He was brilliant, restless, and adventurous. He constantly spent his times on old tales of lost treasures and sunken ruins. I joined him on many adventures, and together we hunted ancient artifacts. This last artifact was different... he devised a plan to retrieve it from the depths of the ocean. Looking back now, it should have stayed there. It was an amulet that he claimed belonged to **Celestia** herself, but it was infected with darkness."*

*"The darkness consumed him. He brought his three children with him to **Portsmith** — already infected, already wracked with that same darkness. They slaughtered the people, struck down the King and the Queen, and took the **Royal Palace** for their own. Our **Royal Guard** did not stand a chance. The few survivors suffer the most... he infected them with the darkness and they now do his bidding."*

He looks down at his weathered gauntlets. *"I carry the shame of what happened every day. I confronted him pleading with him to stop what he was doing. He turned to me and told me that his friend was no more, that he is now the **Dark Lord Malekor** and struck me with his sword. A few **Royal Guard** came to assist me but he had a dark shield around him making him impervious; he cut them down easily. I ran away like a coward and found refuge with some folks in the church basement."*

He shakes his head, turns away and walks towards the back wall of the room, too ashamed to face you. The priest sitting on the floor rises and lays a steadying hand on his shoulder before turning to the party.

From a worn cloth pouch at her hip she draws a small object and presses it into your palm. *"Take this key. It will let you into a safe inside the home of **Sir Roderick**. As the leader of the **Royal Guard**, he holds the key that will let you access the **Armory** at the **Royal Guard Barracks**."* She walks you over to the staircase and whispers, *"**Sir Roderick** tells me that the **Armory** contains a secret passage into the **Royal Palace**."*

> 📜 The key opens a safe that can be found within the home of **Sir Roderick**, which has a the key to the **Armory** within the **Royal Guard Barracks** that can be used to gain access to a secret passage that takes you into the **Royal Palace**.

### 📍 The Royal Guard Barracks

You make your way to the **Royal Guard Barracks**, a walled yard where the castle's soldiers once drilled. The area is filled with dented shields and pitted swords, a sign that the **Royal Guards** put up a fight before falling. The yard contains several smaller buildings used by the **Royal Guard** including the **Armory**. It currently is being guarded a group of **Dark Royal Guards**, consumed by darkness to be obedient.


### 🗺️ The Watchful Yard

You observe the group of **Dark Royal Guard** patrolling the yard. They move in a slow, deliberate circuit of the yard and their hollow eyes sweep the same beats, then rest — and that is the window the party can use to move.

> 📜 This is a short, stealth-based mini game. The **Dark Royal Guard** patrol the entire **Royal Guard Barracks**. A stealth challenge lets the party reach the armory unseen; failing it triggers a battle. The party needs a key unlock the **Armory** door, which holds a secret passage into the **Royal Palace**. Each party member who crosses the yard makes a **DC 12 Dexterity (Stealth) Check** as they move. On a success, they pass unseen. On a failure, a patrolling **Royal Guard** spots them and the ambush begins.
>
> If the party reaches the **Armory** door without the key, they will discover it is locked and must sneak back out to find the key elsewhere.

### 📍 The Armory Passage

Inside the armory, past the bare racks and the fallen gear, a section of the back wall is set with a heavy iron door — the one from **Roderick**'s note. **Roderick's Armory Key** turns in its lock, and the door grinds open on a narrow, cold passage that runs along the bedrock beneath the palace's eastern wing.

The passage is empty of guards. The fallen never thought to watch a way in that was never meant to be used. It climbs, step by step, into a shadowed servants' stair that opens directly into the lower halls of the **Royal Palace** — well behind the courtyard and its heavy dark-creatures.

> 📜 Reaching this passage lets the party bypass the **Castle Gatekeeper Vanguard** and the **Abyssal Dread-Knight** holding the palace courtyard. It is the stealthy route into the **Great Hall**.

### 📍 The Living Quarters

You move through the **Living Quarters**, a tangle of residential blocks whose original beauty is buried under ash and grime. Doors hang off their hinges, and the shutters have warped in the heat. A curtain twitches. A shadow shifts. Nothing moves openly, but nothing is truly still either.

The party is searching for **Sir Roderick**'s home, following the priest's direction with the **Brass Key** in hand. As they work from door to door, the darkness stirs and follows.

> 📜 For each home the party enters, roll **1d6**: on a **1–5** they stumble into the matching random battle, and on a **6** they find the correct home. The correct home is not marked, so the party keeps moving and re-rolls after each battle. The party may continue searching after each battle unless destroyed, but every failed roll is a fight they must survive.

> 💡 Give the players one strong clue per wrong door they rule out — a portrait of another family, a child's toy, a different smell — so the search feels purposeful rather than random.

### 📍 Sir Roderick's Home

You try the **Brass Key** in the lock, and for the first time since entering the town, it turns without resistance. The door gives way onto a small, dust-covered sitting room. A family portrait hangs crooked above the mantel, and a single bed stands made in the corner — clearly the home of the knight who now shelters in the church below.

On the narrow writing desk, tucked beneath a stack of faded letters, lies a second key on a worn leather cord: **Roderick's Armory Key**. Beside it, a folded note in a steady hand reads: *The armory door, back wall, left of the training yard. Behind that door is the only way into the palace that none of the fallen ever guarded. Forgive me — I could not use it before now.*

> 📜 **Roderick's Armory Key** unlocks the armory in the **Royal Guard Barracks**. The party may pocket the key and return to it, but the correct home is the only one that holds it. Finding it flags the **Roderick's Redemption** narrative branch for the chapter's ending.

### 📍 The Royal Palace

You stand before the **Royal Palace**, a vast structure of black stone crowning the hill above **Portsmith**. Its great doors are sealed tight with dark runes, and the air in front of them hums with a low, cold pressure. The palace looms over the ruined town, and it is plain to see that the darkness has poured out from here.

Between the party and those doors lies the palace courtyard — a wide, open killing ground. The fallen **Royal Guard** hold the walkways in slow, patient ranks, and at the center of the yard a single colossal figure waits with its back turned to the doors: the **Abyssal Dread-Knight** from the ascent, the last and heaviest guardian **Malakor** set to stop anyone who would storm the palace by force.

> 📜 This is the direct route into the **Royal Palace**: fight through the **Castle Gatekeeper Vanguard** and the **Abyssal Dread-Knight** in the courtyard. The **Armory Passage** in the **Royal Guard Barracks** is the alternate, stealthy route that bypasses this courtyard entirely and drops the party into the lower halls instead.

## 💬 The Manor & Sir Roderick

Inside the dusty, cobweb-covered grand foyer of **Malek**'s manor, you find a towering man in tarnished plate armor kneeling before a fireplace. This is **Sir Roderick the Unyielding**, **Malek**'s former companion-in-arms.

The warrior rises slowly, his hand resting on the pommel of a battered longsword. His face is lined with deep grief and pale with fear, though his posture betrays elite combat prowess.

*"More victims for the butcher's block?"* **Roderick** asks, his voice trembling slightly. *"No... you have the light of the shrines upon you."*

He sinks back against a dust-covered wooden table, looking up at a faded family portrait hanging above the mantel. It depicts a handsome, smiling man standing beside a gentle woman, surrounded by three young, happy children.

*"Our king and queen were just rulers, but **Malakor** corrupted his own children—**Ves**, **Cal**, and **Nevin**—and unleashed them upon the castle. They overthrew the crown. When the townspeople tried to flee to the ships, **Malakor** slayed them all in cold blood."*

## 💬 The Great Hall

You burst into the sprawling, cathedral-like throne room of the castle. High above, a massive circular skylight frames the moon, now reaching its full phase. At the center of the hall, resting upon a raised stone altar, lies the unconscious, glowing form of your **Angel Goddess Mother**. Standing over her is a towering figure wrapped in dark, shadowy armor—**Dark Lord Malakor**, holding a dagger pulsing with dark void energy.

Before you can step forward, the heavy iron castle doors slam shut behind you with a deafening *CRASH*, sealed tight by dark runes. There is no retreat now.

**Malakor** doesn't even turn around. He glances back over his shoulder, a translucent, impenetrable **Abyssal Shield** surrounding his body. *"The full moon reaches its zenith,"* his voice echoes like grinding stone. *"Childish pests... you are moments too late—the ritual begins the instant her blood is spilled. Children! Dispose of them!"*

**Vespera**, **Calix**, and **Nil** step forward in unison, drawing their weapons. Though their aura is visibly dimmed and weakened by the cleansed orb shrines, fighting all three together presents a formidable wall of steel, blood, and shadow.

> 📜 Any player attempt to attack or move toward **Malakor** during this phase automatically fails against his impenetrable **Abyssal Shield**. Focus strictly on the three siblings!

## 💬 Malakor Unbound

Upon the defeat of the three siblings, they collapse to the stone floor, their dark forms dissolving into harmless smoke.

### 💬 Divine Respite

As the final sibling falls, silence descends upon the inner sanctum. On the altar above, the bound figure of your mother, **Elora**, begins to pulse with a warm, brilliant golden radiance. Though she remains unconscious, her divine power surges outward in a gentle wave of light that washes over each of you. Your wounds stitch close, your exhaustion evaporates, and your magical energy surges back to full strength.

> 📜 **Easy / Normal Difficulty:** The divine pulse grants the entire party the full benefits of a Long Rest (restoring all Hit Points, Spell Slots, and limited-use class features).
>
> **Restock Exception:** Consumable items (such as **Cora**'s apothecary salves) are **not** restocked by this effect.
>
> **Hard Mode (Optional):** If playing on Hard Difficulty, omit this divine pulse. The party receives no rest benefits before confronting **Malakor**, carrying over whatever resources remain from the fight with the siblings.

**Malakor** turns slowly, staring down at the dissolved remnants of his children with utter disdain. *"Useless fools,"* he sneers, stepping off the dais as his **Abyssal Shield** dissipates into thin air. *"Defeated by mortals. No matter. The ritual is nearly complete, and I will tear your souls apart with my own hands!"*

> 📜 With his shield shattered and his children fallen, **Malakor**'s defense drops to AC 20.

## 💬 The Climax & Shattering The Amulet

When **Malakor** reaches 0 HP, he staggers backward toward the altar, but refuses to yield.

He drops to one knee, coughing up dark obsidian blood. Above, the clouds part completely, and a radiant beam of moonlight shines through the high skylight, illuminating the motionless, glowing form of your mother on the altar.
