# D&D: The Tides of Fate

## 💬 The Shattered Tides

The sea thunders against the hull of your vessel as you cut through towering, dark ocean swells. Ahead of you, illuminated by lightning and shrouded in unnatural black mist, lies the dread flagship of **Dark Lord Malakor**. 

The **Dark Lord** kidnapped your mother, taking her to his island. You and your siblings have pursued him across the sea in order to rescue her. You push your ship to its absolute limit, drawing alongside his flagship. Iron grapples fly through the storm, locking the two vessels together. Weapons drawn, you leap across the gap onto the wooden deck, intent on cutting down the warlord and bringing your mother home.

Standing at the helm, clad in jagged, tide-carved plate armor, Malakor turns to face you. A pulsing, obsidian pendant, the **Amulet of the Abyss**, glows against his chest. Wrapped around him is a swirling barrier of impenetrable dark-energy: **The Abyssal Shield**. He unsheaths his great sword.

"Fools," Malakor sneers, his voice echoing with demonic resonance. "You rush to your deaths like waves crashing against a cliff."

## ⚔️ The Unwinnable Assault

> 📜 This is a narrative-driven, guaranteed defeat encounter. It establishes Malakor's overwhelming threat before shattering the party's ship. Malakor's barrier makes him immune to all damage. Once he demonstrates his power, he destroys the party's ship with an elemental blast, casting the heroes into the ocean.

### Stats: Dark Lord Malakor (Full Shielded Power)

| **Speed**                   | 50 ft, Swim 50 ft           | **Damage Immunities**       | All Damage (Abyssal Shield) |
| **Hit Points**              | ???                         | **Condition Immunities**    | ALL Conditions              |

### Actions & Abilities
* **Multiattack:** Malakor makes two *Abyssal Greatsword* attacks.
* **Abyssal Greatsword:** +12 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 2d8 + 7 slashing damage + 2d6 cold damage.
* **Abyssal Blast (Recharge 5–6 / Scripted Round 2–3):** A wave of dark arcana explodes outward in a 30-ft cone. Targets must make a **DC 18 STR Save** or take 4d10 force damage and be pushed 20 ft back.

### DM Reaction & Event Rules
* **When Players Attack Malakor:** Any attack or spell that hits Malakor bounces harmlessly off **The Abyssal Shield**. Describe the spell dissolving into dark water or the blade recoiling with a harsh metallic ring.
* **Round 1:** Malakor strikes with his sword at the frontliner, demonstrating his raw physical threat (+12 to hit).
* **Round 2:** Malakor unleashes *Abyssal Blast* or strikes another player, making it clear their standard tactics cannot break his protection.
* **Round 3 (Climax):** Malakor raises his sword, channeling the dark power of the Amulet of the Abyss. A massive pillar of water and dark energy erupts underneath the party's ship, snapping its main mast and shattering the hull into splinters.

## 💬 A Defeated Party

The deck violently buckles beneath your feet as Malakor’s dark wave rips your vessel apart. Splintered timber explodes through the air, and the roar of the ocean swallows your battle cries. 

Plunged into the frigid, turbulent sea, you claw your way to the surface just in time to watch Malakor’s flagship glide effortlessly through the storm, pulling away into the impenetrable gloom. Locked in chains at the stern, your mother reaches out a hand toward you before she is dragged out of sight.

Clinging to floating debris, battered and exhausted, your strength rapidly fades. The storm rages around you, cold water fills your lungs, and the vast, empty ocean threatens to pull you under into the deep. 

As darkness takes your vision, all hope of saving your mother sinks into the tide...
