# 🔱 The Tides of Fate (Prologue)

## 💬 The Shattered Tides

The sea thunders against the hull of your vessel as you cut through towering, dark ocean swells. Ahead of you, illuminated by lightning and shrouded in unnatural black mist, lies the dreaded flagship of **Dark Lord Malekor**. 

The **Dark Lord** kidnapped your mother, taking her to his island. You and your siblings have pursued him across the sea in order to rescue her. You push your ship to its absolute limit, drawing alongside his flagship until the two vessels drift side by side, close enough to leap between. Weapons drawn, you cross the gap and land on his wooden deck, intent on cutting down the warlord and bringing your mother home.

Standing at the helm, clad in jagged, tide-carved plate armor, **Malekor** turns to face you. A pulsing, obsidian pendant, the **Amulet of the Abyss**, glows against his chest. Wrapped around him is a swirling barrier of impenetrable dark-energy — **The Abyssal Shield**. He unsheaths his great sword, and before you can even plant your feet, he raises it toward the sky. A bolt of black lightning crashes down from the roiling clouds and strikes your vessel dead center, splintered timber scattering across the water as it breaks apart and sinks. He has left you no escape.

*"Fools,"* **Malekor** sneers, his voice echoing with demonic resonance. *"You rush to your deaths like waves crashing against a cliff."*

## ⚔️ The Unwinnable Assault

> 📜 This is a narrative-driven, guaranteed defeat encounter. It establishes **Malekor**'s overwhelming threat. **Malekor**'s barrier makes him immune to all damage. The moment you board, he wrecks your ship to cut off your escape, and once he demonstrates his power, he hurls the heroes into the sea with no way home.

### Battle Actions

* 🗡️ **Multiattack:** **Malekor** makes two *Abyssal Greatsword* attacks.
* 🗡️ **Abyssal Greatsword:** Guaranteed hit. *Hit:* 2d8 + 7 slashing damage + 2d6 cold damage.
* 👑 **Abyssal Blast:** Guaranteed hit. A wave of dark arcana explodes outward in a 30-ft cone. Targets take 4d10 force damage and are pushed 20 ft back.
* 👑 **Abyssal Judgment:** A colossal wave of pure dark energy detonates outward from **Malekor** in all directions. No saving throw is required; the effect is absolute and narrative, hurling every target clean off his deck and into the sea. Distinct from **Abyssal Blast** — this is his ultimate, all-consuming release.
* ⚡ **Automatic Reaction:** Any attack or spell that hits **Malekor** bounces harmlessly off **The Abyssal Shield**. Describe the spell as dissolving into dark water or the blade recoiling with a harsh metallic ring.

### Battle Narration

* **Round 1:** **Malekor** uses **Multiattack** on the frontline party member.
* **Round 2:** **Malekor** uses **Abyssal Blast**, making it clear their standard tactics cannot break his protection.
* **Round 3:** **Malekor** uses **Abyssal Judgment** — a colossal shockwave of dark energy erupts from his body that hurls the party off his ship and into the sea.

## 💬 A Defeated Party

Growing impatient, **Malekor** declares, *"I grow tired of you."* Dark energy spirals inward, coiling around him until the air itself seems to scream. Then, in a single breath, he releases it all — a colossal shockwave detonating outward from his body catches every one of you and hurls you clean off his ship. The roar of the sea swallows your battle cries as you plummet into the churning dark below.

Plunged into the frigid, turbulent sea, you claw your way to the surface just in time to watch his flagship glide effortlessly through the storm, pulling away into the impenetrable gloom. Clinging to floating debris, battered and exhausted, your strength rapidly fades. The storm rages around you, cold water fills your lungs, and the vast, empty sea threatens to pull you under into the deep. 

As darkness takes your vision, all hope of saving your mother sinks into the tide...
