# Chapter 2.1: Emerald Island

## 💠 Chapter Overview

> 📜 **Emerald Island** was named for its emerald-canopied forests, sprawling mountain peaks, and crystal waterfalls. At the center of the pristine lake stands the ancient **Shrine Of Life**, sheltering the **Orb Of Life**. The island has fallen into ruin following the arrival of **Calix The Flesh-Rotter**, who has corrupted the Celestial relic and infected the island with necrotic decay.
>
> 🌴 **Corroding Sludge:** *(Environmental Hazard)*
> While the **Orb Of Life** remains corrupted, each creature on the island takes **1d4 Necrotic Damage** at the start of its turn, and any hit points restored by spells or traits are halved. **Cora's Sea-Salve** is immune to this healing reduction.
>
> 🌟 **Radiance Of Life:** *(Celestial Blessing)*
> Cleansing the **Orb Of Life** grants the party the **Radiance Of Life** Celestial Blessing. Each party member gains an additional one-time **1d10 Maximum HP** increase. Additionally, at the start of each of their turns in combat, each party member regains **1d4 Hit Points**.
>
> ✨ **Finn's Scouting Intel:** If the party selects **Finn** as their **Companion**, then provide them a full scouting map, which includes additional details.

## 💬 Arrival

The sea turns murky green as you close in on a jagged island of dying mangroves and black sludge. The air is thick with the stench of sulfur and rot, stinging your eyes and coating the back of your throat with a sharp, acrid taste. **Captain Tobias** presses a damp rag over his mouth and nose, his weathered face grim behind the cloth.

*"Breathe through your sleeves, lads. This place is pure rot. I'm dropping you off at the rock ledge, then pulling back to open waters before this muck eats through my ship's hull. Signal me when the air clears and I'll pick you up."*

The moment your boots hit the blackened rock, the smell hits you in full — a wall of putrid, chemical decay that makes your stomach clench. The island stretches out ahead of you in a tangle of sickly mangroves and gray, lifeless earth. The soil is slick and iridescent, glistening with a film of black bile that seems to pulse with a slow, toxic breath.

You make your way toward the shrine at the center of what was once a beautiful, pristine lake fed by a clear river. But the lake is beyond recognition. Where clear water once lapped gently against the shore, a thick, bubbling sludge now churns in sickly shades of green and black. It hisses where it meets the stone. The wooden docks have warped and blackened, their planks soft and crumbling at the touch. The boats that once rocked in the current sit sunk and half-dissolved in the acidic murk, their hulls split open and weeping.

Standing at the entrance of the shrine is **Calix The Flesh-Rotter**. He is a towering brute clad in rusted plate mail that weeps black, acidic sludge from every seam, the metal pitted and groaning under its own corruption. His eyes burn with a feverish, relentless rage, and each labored step he takes sends a fresh trickle of black bile pooling at his feet.

*"Pain... everything is pain!"* he roars, his voice a ragged, broken thing, cracking like old timber. *"You will feel the pain that I feel."*

Without another word, he turns and stumbles into the shrine. The heavy stone doors grind shut behind him with a final, earth-shaking thud, sealing his anguish and the **Orb Of Life** inside.

> 📜 The party is now stranded on the island, facing a toxic lake that blocks direct access to the shrine. They must explore the surrounding terrain to find another way in.

### 💬 The Toxic Lake

Before you can gather your thoughts, a boar bursts from the mangroves, bristles raised and eyes wild. A **Corpse-Sludge Crawler** pursues it, jaws snapping, a high and reedy shriek tearing from its maw.

The boar's headlong sprint carries it toward the corroded docks. Its hooves, heavy and panicked, give way and it slips. Then the boards break, and the boar plunges into the sludge. You have a clear, unobstructed view of what happens next. For one heartbeat, the boar thrashes with a brief, desperate convulsion. Then the sludge hisses, and the creature begins to dissolve from the inside out. Black bile bubbles up in thick, oily columns around its flanks. The bones, or what's left of them, float to the surface for a moment before sinking and vanishing into the churning murk. The lake glistens where the boar fell. The stench lingers, sharp and chemical, and the water where it dissolved is a shade darker than the rest.

The **Corpse-Sludge Crawler** halts at the edge of the docks. Its shriek cuts off. It turns, its jaws parting to reveal row upon row of needle-thin, rotting teeth, and locks onto you.

> 📜 This opening is meant to demonstrate that the lake is not merely a barrier but an active, corrosive hazard which will cause death.

### ⚔️ A Toxic Creature

🐉 [Corpse-Sludge Crawler]

> 📜 This opening battle provides a key feature to communicate to the party: the monsters on this island may have a death reaction which causes them to erupt in a burst of acidic sludge when reduced to 0 HP. This sludge causes damage to the party and is concrete proof that even a victory comes at a cost.

## 🗺️ Exploration

> 📜 Players are free to explore the island. The large mountains and deep forests make it difficult to observe where to head to first.
>
> 🌴 If the party attempts to swim across the lake, they take **1d6 + 4 Acid Damage** at the start of each round they remain in the water. After each round, damage increases by another **1d6 Acid Damage** per round with no ceiling. Describe the burning, the stinging, the slow erosion of skin and armor. Warn them clearly that staying in the water will kill them.

* **The Shrine Lake**
* **The Shore**
* **The Western Plateau**

* **The Northern Mountains**

### 📍 The Shrine Lake

You head back to the lake hoping to gain any other insight, but when you arrive you discover that there isn't anything more to gain. The shrine seems so close, yet remains so far. If not for the toxicity of the lake, you could simply swim across. You realize the solution to this problem is somewhere out there.

### 📍 The Shore

You head back to shore hoping to find some clues as to what to do. **The Salty Sea-Dog** is completely out of sight, eliminating the possibility of asking for aid. It seems as if there is nothing here that can help you reach the shrine.

### 📍 The Western Plateau

You head to the **Western Plateau** and are standing in front of a very steep, narrow trail carved into the cliff face. The stone is slick with the island's toxic residue, handholds are loose and crumbling, and a misstep means a long drop back down. Staring at the steep cliff face, you are a bit uneasy. Do you climb to the top or do you turn around and try to find another path?

> 📜 If the party decides to climb to the top, each player must succeed on three **DC 14 STR (Athletics) checks**. A failed check means the player slips, takes **1d6 Bludgeoning Damage**, and must retry that check. Climbing down afterward does not require any checks.
>
> The climb is intentionally difficult but not lethal. **The Western Plateau** is a risk and the intel gained here is valuable, but the cost is time and potentially a small amount of health.

At the top, the wind picks up and the stench thins out. The entire island spreads out below and you notice things that were more difficult to see from the ground. You spot a waterfall of dark, sludgy water in the **Northern Mountains**. It seems the source of the river begins at this waterfall and takes a long and winding path down into the **Deep Forest** and eventually into the **Shrine Lake**.

Nestled at the base of the waterfall, half-hidden by twisted trees, is a small **Stone Cabin**. Moored beside it at the river's edge is a boat with a dark, seamless hull that carries a faint, shifting iridescence. The sludge swirls around it without clinging. Even from this distance, it looks unaffected by the toxicity of the sludge.

> 📜 If the party has **Finn's Scouting Intel**, this confirms what they already know. Without it, this is the first visual evidence that a solution to cross the lake safely exists.

### 📍 The Deep Forest

You head into the **Deep Forest** following the shoreline along the river. It is a wall of black-barked trees, their canopies so thick that the light filtering through is green and sickly. The ground is soft, spongy, and coated in a film of black spore. The air is heavy, still, and dead. There are no birds and no insects, just the slow drip of toxic moisture from the leaves.

The river runs through the heart of the forest, its banks narrow and overgrown. The path following the river upstream is treacherous. The trail is half-swallowed by roots, the ground is slick with rot, and the river itself is a constant, hissing presence at your heels.

> 📜 The **Deep Forest** is the most dangerous route to take. The river winds in long, serpentine curves, and the path is poorly defined. It should feel claustrophobic and hostile. As the party progresses upstream, the dark beasts grow bolder and the path narrows. The contrast when they finally emerge onto the open ridge of the **Northern Mountains** should feel like surfacing from deep water.
>
> 🌴 The toxic river is active. Creatures in the water will attack the party as they navigate the banks.
>
> 💡 Use your best judgement to determine how many random battles the party faces on their way to the **Northern Mountains** until they reach the stone cabin.

#### ⚔️ Random Battles (Deep Forest River Bank)

* 🐉 [Necrotic Shark] (A), 🐉 [Necrotic Shark] (B), 🐉 [Necrotic Shark] (C)
* 🐉 [Rotted Harpy] (A), 🐉 [Rotted Harpy] (B), 🐉 [Dark Mastiff]
* 🐉 [Toxic River Beast]

### 📍 The Northern Mountains

As you stand before the **Northern Mountains** you feel that the air here is slightly less noxious, though the path is still hazardous.

As you crest the ridge, you discover a small, weathered **Stone Cabin** tucked beside a waterfall feeding the river. Beside it, you find a boat in pristine condition. Up close, its dark, seamless hull feels unnaturally smooth beneath your hand. Where the toxic river laps against its sides, the water recoils without clinging, suggesting that the boat is made from some kind of enchanted wood. The boat is intact, dry, and waiting.

> 📜 This is the convergence point. Regardless of which route the party took — the **Western Plateau**, the **Deep Forest**, or a direct approach through the **Northern Mountains**, the enchanted boat is the solution to crossing the lake. Once the party has the boat, they can ride it down the river.
>
> If the party explores the **Stone Cabin**, they will startle the **Mountain Stalker** inside and it will cause a battle.
> 
> 🌴 While riding the boat down the river, the party will encounter hostile dark beasts in the water. The boat's hull protects the party from the toxic water itself, but the dark beasts are a separate threat.

#### ⚔️ The Beast Inside (Stone Cabin)

You walk toward the cabin and open the door. A **Mountain Stalker** runs outside and toward the river, then stops abruptly. It turns around and stares at you. It slowly begins to walk toward you, stomping the ground beneath it to show that it means to attack you for disturbing it.

🐉 [Mountain Stalker]

The mountain stalker falls with a final blow and screeches out. Thankfully, it is not as toxic as the other dark beasts. You observe the cabin door remains open. Do you choose to enter or change your mind after this encounter?

#### 💬 The Stone Cabin

After defeating the **Mountain Stalker**, you decide to enter the **Stone Cabin** and see what else is inside. The stench of rot is thick, but beneath it lingers a faint, clean scent of dried herbs and ink. It seems that someone once lived in this cabin. A writing desk sits against the far wall, buried under scrolls and half-finished notes. A single dead oil lamp guards a leather journal, its final page still open. The shelves hold rows of bottled reagents and anatomical sketches, and in the corner a workbench holds three vials of **Potion of Greater Healing** and something you have never seen before: a vial of crystal-blue water with a golden radiance glowing from within it.

> 📜 The journal entry, a ruined workbench, and a dark beast that claimed the researcher's home all point to one conclusion: the researcher was killed by the beast.
>
> * **Vial Of Celestial Water:** *(Wondrous Item, Unique)* A small glass vial of luminous, slow-turning liquid that glows with a warm, inner light, distilled from the divine light of the **Orb Of Life**. The vial is a single-use item and can deal **8d8 Radiant Damage**.
> * **Potion of Greater Healing:** A small glass vial that restores **4d4 + 4 HP** when consumed.
>
> 💡 The cabin is an optional detour, not a forced encounter. Reward the party for choosing to explore and clearing the cabin. The **Vial Of Celestial Water** will be very useful against **Calix The Flesh-Rotter**, but it can also be saved and used in other battles.

#### 💬 The Final Journal Entry

You head to the desk to read the journal. It lies open with the final entry in view.

*"After years on this island researching **Celestia** and the **Orb Of Life**, I have managed to capture the divine light from the very essence of the **Orb Of Life**. I now possess a **Vial Of Celestial Water**. I originally intended to provide this as a gift to the world, but darkness descended onto this island in recent days. The waters and land have become toxic, and I feel that the beasts of this island have grown darker and more aggressive. I constructed a boat made from **Celestial Wood** in hopes of using it to escape if such a need arises. I also thought of using this vial against a beast that has been stalking me, but I believe it is too important to use it on such a lowly beast. If it ever must be spent, let it be spent against the true rot that devoured this place and corrupted the **Orb Of Life**. I only hope I find safety within these cabin walls. **Celestia**, I pray to you for assistance."*

It seems obvious now; the beast that resided inside was the same that stalked the researcher. You hold a moment of silence for the researcher before continuing on.

## 💬 Riding The Boat

You head onto the enchanted boat and it slides off the river's edge to catch the current with a low, resonant hum. The moment it is underway, the calm gives way. The water churns into a series of steep rapids, each drop a fresh plunge that throws the little craft sideways against the banks and twists the current into cold, black whirlpools. The stench of the river rises off the water in hot, acrid waves, and you can feel the hull shudder under the strain of the fall.

Halfway down the river, the current suddenly stills. The water widens into a short, black stretch, and from its depths rises a great, slow heave. Something is moving beneath the surface. A dark beast emerges and turns toward the boat with a low, wet growl that rattles in the chest, and the enchanted wood beneath you hums in a low, warning note. Then another appears, and another. They swim toward the boat.

> 📜 This is a short, fast-paced mini game built around a **DC 13 DEX (Acrobatics)** check for each party member. The checks represent the crew wrestling the tiller, bracing against the rapids, and steering the enchanted boat as it barrels down the river toward the beasts.
>
> * **100% (All Party Members) Succeed**
> * **At Least 50% Succeed**
> * **Less Than 50% Succeed**
>
> 🌴 The enchanted hull protects the party from the river itself, but the creature and its death-burst are separate threats. The burst is a key reminder for the party: even a clean victory on this island comes at a cost.
>
> 💡 Gauge how many checks each party member makes based on your judgement: the number of rapids you want to narrate, the party's level, and the current difficulty tier. A larger or more skilled party may benefit from fewer checks per player so the result still feels like a genuine test rather than a foregone conclusion; a struggling party may benefit from an extra check to swing the outcome.

### 💬 100% Succeed (All Party Members)

You work the tiller in perfect unison. The enchanted boat heels hard across the current, threading a narrow gap between the beast's jaws and the bank. The beast lunges, its maw snapping shut on empty water, and the boat rockets past it.

### 💬 At Least 50% Succeed

You lose some control, but the enchanted boat still has momentum. Instead of steering clear, the bow drives straight into the creature. The impact is catastrophic. The beast is crushed beneath the enchanted hull and dies on the spot. As it dies, it erupts in a burst of black, acidic sludge that spatters onto you.

> 📜 Each party member must make a **DC 14 DEX Saving Throw** or take **1d6 Acid Damage** (half on success).

### 💬 Less Than 50% Succeed

The boat is wrenched off course, and the beasts jump onboard. The enchanted wood is protected from the sludge, but the same cannot be said of you. The dark beast attacks you.

## 💬 The Shrine Of Life: The Orb Of Life

The enchanted boat shudders to a halt as the current gentles and the river finally opens out onto the great, sickly lake. Beyond the blackened docks and the half-dissolved hulls of a dozen dead boats, the shrine stands at the center of the lake. A squat, ancient structure of dark stone, its walls weeping the same black bile that coats the rest of the island. Its doors are the heavy stone slabs that **Calix The Flesh-Rotter** sealed behind himself when he made his way inside, and they have not moved since.

You take a moment to catch your breath, then approach. The doors are cold to the touch, and faintly warm in places, as if something inside is still burning. When they are finally pried and pulled apart, they groan open on rusted hinges, and the stench from within is tenfold worse.

Inside, the shrine is a single, vaulted chamber. The **Orb Of Life** floats at its center, pulsing with a dull, sickly green light that has long since curdled. And standing before it is **Calix The Flesh-Rotter**. He turns, his feverish eyes locking onto yours, and raises his rotting battle axe with a ragged, broken snarl.

*"You want the pain?"* he roars. *"Then feel it with me."* He moves toward you, ready to swing his axe and take your head clean off. Two specters appear behind you. There is no escape. You must defeat him and cleanse the shrine of this darkness.

### ⚔️ Calix The Flesh-Rotter (Mini-Boss)

🐉 [Specter Minion] (A), 🐉 [Specter Minion] (B), 👹 [Calix The Flesh-Rotter]

> 📜 The party is only required to defeat **Calix The Flesh-Rotter**, and not any of the **Specter Minion** beasts.

## 💬 The Cleansing

**Calix The Flesh-Rotter** staggers and falls to his knees, his rotting battle axe slipping from his grip and clattering onto the stone floor. As you step forward to deliver the final blow, the dark energy bound within his chest flares in a violent, protective surge. Black, oily tendrils burst from his armor, deflecting your strike.

Directly beneath his knees, the flagstones bleed into a yawning rift of pure shadow and dark flames flicker up from the mouth of the portal. The darkness cradles his injured frame, pulling him downward. His eyes fix on you with a lingering glare as he sinks into the void.

The portal snaps shut behind him with a silent pulse, leaving no trace behind; the stone floor remains completely undamaged.

The **Orb Of Life** shudders. The sickly green at its core flickers, gutters, and then blooms into a warm, radiant light that warms the chamber. The light spills through the open doors and across the blackened stone, and where it touches, the island begins to heal. The black bile across the shrine walls dries and flakes off, revealing clean, pale stone beneath. Beyond the doors, you can see the change spreading: the churning sludge of the great lake calming, the blackened docks lightening, and the sickly mangroves straightening as fresh, green life creeps back into their leaves. The stench of sulfur and rot, which has clung to the back of your throats since you landed, finally thins and lifts into the clean, salt-tinged air.

The **Orb Of Life** settles into a steady, green pulse at the center of the shrine, and its light wraps gently around you, warm and steady. A new strength settles into you, a quiet, constant warmth that will follow you for the rest of the journey.

Having seen the flash of light emanating from the shrine, **Captain Tobias** takes this as the signal. He returns to the island to pick you up. You make your way back to shore and board **The Salty Sea-Dog**.

**Captain Tobias** greets you as you board **The Salty Sea-Dog**, leaning heavily on the railing. He pulls off his damp face rag, taking a deep, unhurried breath of the fresh ocean air. A wide grin breaks across his face. *"By the gods, lads... I felt it all the way out on the open water,"* **Captain Tobias** says, shaking his head in disbelief as he gestures toward the lush, blooming coastline. *"The stink vanished in an instant, replaced by a warmth that settled right in my bones. Whatever you did, you brought this place back to life."*

> 📜 The **Orb Of Life** is now cleansed. The negative localized effect on this island is removed, and the island is completely safe. The party now carries the **Radiance Of Life** Celestial Blessing, which provides an instant one-time **1d10 Maximum HP** increase. Additionally, at the start of each of their turns in combat, each party member regains **1d4 Hit Points**.
