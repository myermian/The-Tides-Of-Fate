# Chapter 2.4: Farro Island

## 💠 Farro Island

> 📜 **Farro Island** is the island which holds the town of **Tidefall**, and it is the first to have fallen to the darkness. It is the hometown of **Malekor**, and its close proximity to **Portsmith** is no coincidence; it sits in the shadow of the tempest that now churns over his stronghold.
>
> 💡 Let the strangeness accumulate. The more the party speaks to the townsfolk, the clearer it should become that something is deeply wrong beneath their ordinary manners.

## 💬 Arrival

As **The Salty Sea-Dog** draws toward the island, the sea turns dark and still, and the sky on the far horizon is lit by the slow, rolling thunder of the tempest over **Portsmith**. The storm is close enough to be seen and felt, its edge lashing the water in long, heavy lines. **Farro Island** sits just beyond that line, just out of the storm's reach, and **Tidefall** appears at last: a town of low stone houses, a weathered dock, and a street that looks, for the first few moments, almost ordinary.

**Captain Tobias** grips the wheel and studies the town through the squall on the horizon. His weathered face is grim, and for the first time on this voyage his usual steadiness falters.

*"I'll be honest with you, lads. I don't like how close we are to that storm. The tempest over **Portsmith** is the strongest I've ever felt, and it's only a short pull from here. I've been here before, a nice town, but something about **Tidefall** sits wrong in the gut, like a knot that won't work loose."*

He turns the wheel and eases the ship toward the dock, then looks back at the party over his shoulder.

*"But you've got more of a handle on where this is going than I do, and I've never once doubted your judgment. We're clear of the worst of the storm, and the town is right there, so we're going in. The only question is what we find when we step off."*

**The Salty Sea-Dog** grinds gently against the dock. **Captain Tobias** steps aboard the planks first and looks up at the town, his eyes tracing the rows of houses and the quiet streets behind them.

*"I'm not going up into the town with you, and I don't want the crew leaving the ship either. **Tidefall** gives me a strange feeling, and I can't tell you why it does. It's like the darkness is breathing in here. I'll keep watch over the ship and over the harbor. When you're ready to leave, just return here, we'll be waiting."*

You unboard and step onto the wet planks of the dock. The town stretches out ahead of you, and the first townsfolk you meet greet you with a calm, almost identical welcome. A man nods. A woman offers a small, measured smile. An old fisherman calls out a greeting that a few feet away another fisherman repeats with the same greeting.

> 📜 **Tidefall** has already fallen to the darkness. The townsfolk are not hostile, and they do not need to be; their strangeness is the warning. Keep the truth hidden for now, and let the party discover it at their own pace.
>
> Treat every interaction with the townsfolk as uncanny and unsettling. They are calm, polite, and agreeable in a way that feels rehearsed. They may repeat the same phrases with the same inflection, finish each other's sentences, or watch the party with an attentiveness that is just a shade too focused. The townsfolk will constantly deter and prevent the party from visiting **The Hilltop Manor**.
>
> Visiting **The Hilltop Manor** will reveal important information about **Celestia** and the **Ring of Celestia**. In doing so, the townsfolk turn hostile towards the party and the darkness reveals itself in combat. Exploring the rest of the island is no longer optional and the only path forward is in making your way back to the docks to leave the island.

### 📍 The Harbor

You return to the harbor and observe that the **The Salty Sea-Dog** is still moored at the dock, her rigging creaking in the slow off-shore wind. The water around the hull is dark and still, and the sound of the crew is muffled somewhere below deck. The townsfolk look at you, and as you look back at them, they nod and present you with an eery smile.

### 📍 The Town Square

The square is clean, orderly, and almost completely empty. A few townsfolk sit or stand around it, watching the party with mild curiosity. When spoken to, they give nearly identical answers about the island's safety.

* One townsfolk says, *"There is nothing to fear here."* A moment later, another repeats the same sentence with the same cadence.
* If asked about missing neighbors, the townsfolk answer that everyone is *"where they belong."*
* If asked about **Malekor**, their expressions briefly go blank. They then redirect the conversation toward the weather, the harbor, or the party's destination.

> 💡 The townsfolk do not need to lie convincingly. Their unnatural agreement is the warning. A character who watches closely may notice that several townsfolk blink or turn their heads at the same time.

### 📍 The Old Harbor

The harbor contains small fishing boats pulled high onto the shore. Their nets are wet, but no one admits to going out to sea. The water around the dock is dark and unusually still.

An elderly townsfolk offers directions to the town center, then quietly asks whether the party has come *"to wake him."* When questioned, the townsfolk denies having said anything unusual and repeats the directions word for word.

The party can find signs that the island has been cut off from ordinary travel: rotting supplies, abandoned fishing gear, and a ship's log with its final pages torn away. Do not reveal the full cause of the darkness here; the evidence should point toward the town rather than the sea.

### 📍 The Silent Streets

The streets between the square and the home are broad and well maintained, but every house is unnaturally quiet. Townsfolk watch from doorways without calling to one another. Some stand facing walls. Others carry out simple chores long after there is any reason to continue them.

Use one or more of the following encounters as the party passes through:

* A townsfolk asks a character whether they can hear the voice beneath the island. If the character says no, the townsfolk smiles and says, *"You will."*
* A group of townsfolk turns toward the party together. One asks whether they have seen **Malekor**, while another immediately insists that no one on the island remembers him.
* A townsfolk offers the party food, but the food is cold and untouched. If refused, the townsfolk quietly places it on the ground and walks away without looking back.
* A frightened-looking townsfolk tries to warn the party away from the old home. Before they can finish, another townsfolk appears and completes the sentence for them: *"There is nothing there."*

## 🐉 The Dark Beasts Of Farro Island

The island's wild places are inhabited by dark beasts. They move through the overgrown paths and ruined outskirts, keeping close to the town without entering it. Their presence explains some of the island's physical danger, but not the townsfolk's behavior.

### ⚔️ Dark Beast Encounter

The party encounters dark beasts while crossing the island or searching the outskirts. The beasts attack with unnatural coordination, breaking off only when the party reaches the edge of the town.

> 📜 **Encounter purpose:** This battle establishes that Farro Island is dangerous outside the town as well as within it. The beasts should feel like creatures drawn to the same darkness that has overtaken the townsfolk.

### 📍 The Overgrown Outskirts

The outskirts contain abandoned homes, collapsed sheds, and paths swallowed by blackened weeds. The party can find signs that families left in a hurry, but there are no bodies and no clear route away from the island.

Among the remains, the party can discover a fragment of an old map showing Farro Island's position near **Portsmith**. The map confirms that the town once served as a significant settlement rather than an isolated village.

## 🏠 Malekor's Childhood Home

The house stands apart from the rest of the town. Its windows are covered from the inside, and the front door is unlocked. The interior is preserved more carefully than any other building on the island, as though the townsfolk have been maintaining it for years without ever living in it.

The moment the party enters, the town changes. Doors open throughout the surrounding streets. Footsteps gather outside. The townsfolk abandon their calm routines and converge on the house in silence.

### ⚔️ The Townsfolk Attack

The townsfolk attack the party as soon as the party searches the home or attempts to leave it. They fight without fear or self-preservation, moving in coordinated groups and ignoring wounds that would make an ordinary person retreat.

Their behavior makes the truth clear: the people of Farro Island have been overtaken by the darkness. They are not merely frightened or loyal to Malekor. Something has taken hold of them and is using their familiar faces against the party.

> 📜 **Encounter objectives:** The party must survive the attack and search the house. The townsfolk continue to arrive until the party finds a way to escape, barricades the house, or completes the search.

### 📍 The Preserved Study

Behind a concealed panel or beneath the remains of a writing desk, the party finds the study notes of a young **Malekor**, from before he became the Dark Lord. The notes are written with the intensity of someone who believes he has uncovered a secret that everyone else has missed.

The notes reveal the following:

* **Celestia** had taken human form and lived somewhere near her home.
* She had hidden her ring, and Malekor believed that the ring could reveal her true identity.
* She had hidden her amulet deep in the ocean.
* Malekor believed that finding the amulet would lead him to the ring, and that the ring could ultimately reveal who Celestia truly was.

The notes do not identify **Celestia** as **Elora**. They provide a lead toward the truth without giving away the party's mother's identity before the intended revelation.

> 💡 The handwriting becomes more frantic as the notes continue. Malekor's research is not yet the work of a dark lord, but the obsession that will later define him is already visible.

## ❓ Branching Choices

### ❓ The Party Leaves Farro Island Without Searching The Home

The party can leave after exploring the town or fighting the dark beasts. They learn that the island is overtaken by the darkness, but they do not find Malekor's study notes or the lead concerning Celestia's ring and amulet.

### ❓ The Party Searches Malekor's Childhood Home

The townsfolk attack, forcing the party to confront the truth about Farro Island. After surviving the attack and finding the study notes, the party gains the information needed to investigate **White Sand Island** properly.

If the party visits **White Sand Island** after reading the notes, **Captain Tobias** and the companions join the search for the ring. During that search, one of them accidentally discovers the hidden cave, allowing the party to find **Celestia's ring**.

> 📜 If the party visited **White Sand Island** before Farro Island, they could not discover the hidden cave. The study notes provide the missing connection and make the cave discoverable on a later visit.

---

## 💬 Leaving Farro Island

Once the party leaves the island, the townsfolk return to their silent routines. From the water, the town looks almost peaceful again. The house remains the only place where the darkness shows itself openly: every window is now crowded with watching figures.

The party leaves with a clearer understanding of Malekor's history and a new lead concerning **Celestia's ring**. Their next visit to **White Sand Island** can turn the old notes into a path toward the hidden cave.
