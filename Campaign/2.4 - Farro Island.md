# Chapter 2.4: Farro Island

## 💠 Farro Island

> 📜 **Farro Island** is the island that holds the town of **Tidefall**, and it is the first to have fallen to the darkness. It is the hometown of **Malekor**, and its close proximity to **Portsmith** is no coincidence; it sits in the shadow of the tempest that now churns over his stronghold.
>
> The townsfolk, while not hostile, do not seem to be acting normally. They move in a scripted manner, following the same path, pausing at the same moments, and repeating the same phrases. Every interaction with them is uncanny and unsettling. They may repeat the same lines with the same inflection, finish each other's sentences, or watch the party with an attentiveness that is just a shade too focused. The townsfolk will constantly steer the party away from the **Hilltop Manor**.
>
> 💡 Let the strangeness accumulate. The more the party speaks to the townsfolk, the clearer it should become that something is deeply wrong beneath their ordinary manners.

## 💬 Arrival

As **The Salty Sea-Dog** draws toward the island, the sea turns dark and still, and the sky on the far horizon is lit by the slow, rolling thunder of the tempest over **Portsmith**. The storm is close enough to be seen and felt, its edge lashing the water in long, heavy lines. **Farro Island** sits just beyond that line, just out of the storm's reach, and **Tidefall** appears at last: a town of low stone houses, a weathered dock, and a street that looks, for the first few moments, almost ordinary.

**Captain Tobias** grips the wheel and studies the town through the squall on the horizon. His weathered face is grim, and for the first time on this voyage his usual steadiness falters.

*"I'll be honest with you, lads. I don't like how close we are to that storm. The tempest over **Portsmith** is the strongest I've ever felt, and it's only a short pull from here. I've been here before, a nice town, but something about **Tidefall** sits wrong in the gut, like a knot that won't work loose."*

He turns the wheel and eases the ship toward the dock, then looks back at the party over his shoulder.

*"But you've got more of a handle on where this is going than I do, and I've never once doubted your judgment. We're clear of the worst of the storm, and the town is right there, so we're going in. The only question is what we find when we step off."*

**The Salty Sea-Dog** grinds gently against the dock. **Captain Tobias** steps aboard the planks first and looks up at the town, his eyes tracing the rows of houses and the quiet streets behind them.

*"I'm not going up into the town with you, and I don't want the crew leaving the ship either. **Tidefall** gives me a strange feeling, and I can't tell you why it does. It's like the darkness is breathing in here. I'll keep watch over the ship and over the harbor. When you're ready to leave, just return here, we'll be waiting."*

You disembark and step onto the wet planks of the dock. The town stretches out ahead of you, and the first townsfolk you meet greet you with a calm, almost identical welcome. A man nods. A woman offers a small, measured smile. An old fisherman calls out a greeting that a few feet away another fisherman repeats with the same greeting.

## 🗺️ Exploration

> 📜 Players are free to explore the island.
>
> Visiting the **Hilltop Manor** is not possible to do so in a direct manner, so another path must be discovered in order to enter the manor. Entering the **Hilltop Manor** will reveal important information about **Celestia** and the **Ring of Celestia**. Doing so will turn the townsfolk hostile toward the party and expose the darkness beneath the surface. Additionally, exploring the rest of the island is no longer optional, and the only path forward is to make your way back to the docks and leave the island.

* **The Harbor**
* **The Town Square**
* **The Shopping District**
* **The Living Quarters**
* **The Hilltop Manor**
* **The Abandoned Mines**

### 📍 The Harbor

You return to the **Harbor** and observe that the **Salty Sea-Dog** is still moored at the dock, her rigging creaking in the slow off-shore wind. The water around the hull is dark and still, and the sound of the crew is muffled somewhere below deck. The townsfolk look at you, and as you look back at them, they nod and present you with an eerie smile.

### 📍 The Town Square

You enter the **Town Square** and discover that it is as bustling as the town square at **Oakwood**, though it seems a bit off. The fountain at the center is no longer flowing, the water is still and filthy.

You stand by and observe that the townsfolk are performing in a scripted manner; they follow the same path, pause at the same moments, and have the same conversations, evident by the path marks left behind on the ground. The stalls scattered about only carry weathered or broken gear and equipment, or in the case of food, mold-covered, blackened, and rotting. Even if you brought any money to transact with, this would definitely not be the place to purchase anything.

The guards stationed around the **Town Square** seem a bit different as they stare directly at you; their gaze follows you as you walk around the pavilion.

### 📍 The Shopping District

You enter the shopping district and find it quite deserted. The few townsfolk here are either shopkeepers standing behind empty counters or residents walking in the same scripted loop. The shop windows are filthy, coated in grime and streaked with something darker, and through them you can see that the shelves hold almost nothing.

The few shops that still have any stock carry only weathered or broken gear and equipment. The butcher shop and fish monger have a particularly strong odor coming from within; the meat and fish are spoiled and rotten. Even if you brought coin to spend, this would not be the place to purchase anything.

The shopkeepers look up as you pass, nod once, and then return to the same motion, over and over, as though the gesture has been carved into them.

### 📍 The Living Quarters

You make your way to the living quarters, an area of homes whose original beauty is hidden beneath dirt and grime. Some facades are in desperate repair — cracked shutters, sagging porches, and doors that hang slightly off their frames. The streets are empty, and as you walk past the homes it seems that you are being watched from behind the curtains. A curtain twitches. A shadow shifts. Nothing moves openly, but nothing is truly still either.

### 📍 The Hilltop Manor

You head towards the **Hilltop Manor**, a large estate at the top of a hill overlooking the rest of the town. At the base is a metal gate with ten guards standing in front. As you approach the metal gates, one of the guards advises you that the manor is currently unavailable. *"For your safety, please turn around. The manor is currently closed to visitors. Might I suggest visiting our **Shopping District** or our lovely **Town Square**."*


> 📜 Any attempt to enter the **Hilltop Manor** will result in the guards preventing you from doing so, and interacting with any of them causes them to repeat the same phrase verbatim.

### 📍 The Abandoned Mines

You exit the town and explore the hills until you discover an entrance to the **Abandoned Mines**. The entrance is barred by a sign that reads: *"WARNING! The mines are a restricted zone. Any persons found entering will be arrested and prosecuted for trespassing. Please visit our **Shopping District** and our lovely **Town Square** instead."*

#### 💬 Entering The Mines

It takes a bit of effort, but you pry off the boards that sealed the entrance to the mines. You enter the mines, and on your way in, your foot catches on something unyielding. You kneel down to investigate and discover that it is the ribcage remains of a skeleton.

As you stand up and look around, you discover that sprawled across the tunnel floor are the skeletal remains of half a dozen people, huddled close together in a desperate, defensive circle. Their bones are fractured and scarred with claw marks. Tattered clothing clings to some of their remains. Amidst the carnage, one skeleton lies slightly apart, wearing a heavily dented breastplate etched with the distinct guard insignia of **Tidefall**. You wonder who these people were and why one of them wore the armor of the town guards.

Scattered around are boxes of supplies, most of which are destroyed, but a few are still intact. The path further into the mines is too dark to see beyond where you are currently standing.

> 📜 The **Abandoned Mines** is a purposefully dark and short dungeon with a very dangerous dark beast at the end of it. The skeletal remains are the townsfolk who took shelter in the mines when the darkness fell. They were hunted down by the dark beast residing in the deep and ultimately met their demise here. Rummaging through their gear yields a functional lantern to help the party navigate the darkness if they do not possess any spells or equipment that can help them navigate naturally.

### ❓ Branching Choice 1: You Discover A Solution To The Darkness

Having discovered a solution to the darkness, you proceed deeper into the mines. It is a long, winding tunnel. You pass by an elevator that was used to go deeper into the mines, but the gears are completely smashed to pieces. You continue on until you reach a dead end. Suddenly, a screeching noise echoes from behind you. You hear the sound of the elevator crashing down, and the mines shake. You turn around and discover a **Dark Dragon** blocking your path back to the entrance. It has razor-sharp claws and smoke escaping its nostrils, a sign that it can and will breathe fire.

### ❓ Branching Choice 2: You Proceed In Complete Darkness

You decide to proceed onward in the darkness. You continue down the path clumsily, using the walls to guide you along. You reach what seems to be a dead end, though you cannot tell due to the pitch-black darkness. Suddenly, a screeching noise echoes from behind you. You hear the sound of something metal crashing deep below. The mines begin to shake. You sense the presence of a dark beast behind you. You turn around and observe a blast of fire from a **Dark Dragon**, temporarily illuminating the area. The dark beast blocks the path back to the entrance, intent on having you meet the same fate as those at the entrance.

#### ⚔️ The Beast In The Deep

🐉 [Dark Dragon]

> 📜 The **Dark Dragon** stands between the party and the entrance, with their backs against the dead end. There is no chance of escaping this encounter.
>
> 🌴 If the party did not discover a solution to illuminate the mines, they will end up fighting the dark beast in complete darkness, with only momentary visibility when the dragon breathes fire.

### 💬 A Glimmer Of Light

You strike the beast, and it shrieks out in pain. It staggers and collapses onto the nearby wall, causing the ceiling to cave in; in doing so, the wall crumbles, and there is a faint illumination seeping through. As you approach, you discover a path that leads to a large cavern with bioluminescent fauna covering the walls. At the end is a small staircase leading up to a mysterious door.

### 💬 The Mysterious Door

You climb the long, winding staircase to the top and stand before the mysterious door. You decide to open it, and in doing so you discover, of all things, a wine cellar. You enter the cellar only to have the door close behind you, sealing you inside. Attempts to open the door from this side are futile, as it is protected from destruction by some dark magic. At the end of the wine cellar are more stairs, leading up to another room.

## 🗺️ The Hilltop Manor

You make your way upstairs from the wine cellar into the large kitchen. Looking around, it seems that you have found yourself a way into the **Hilltop Manor**. The manor is quite large and contains many rooms, with bedrooms and an office on the upper floor. Nothing in the kitchen immediately seems out of the ordinary. From here you can see through the windows the manor's garden and the accompanying door that leads to the garden. There are also other areas to explore within the manor.

* The Wine Cellar
* The Kitchen
* The Dining Hall
* The Living Room
* The Foyer
* Bedroom (1)
* Bedroom (2)
* Bedroom (3)
* Bedroom (4)
* The Study

> 📜 Any attempt to leave the manor through any of the doors or windows is futile as they are all locked and protected by dark magic. Note that in choosing to explore a bedroom, the party will explore them in order; they do not select a specific bedroom.

### 📍 The Wine Cellar

You head back down into the **Wine Cellar**. Dust coats the racks, and most of the bottles have long since been emptied, shattered, or reduced to dark sediment beneath their corks. The stone walls hold the cold of the earth. The door that led you into the cellar still remains shut tight.

### 📍 The Kitchen

You walk back into the kitchen. Copper pots hang above a broad stone hearth, and long counters run beneath shelves filled with dust-covered jars. The room was once well cared for, but grime has settled into every corner and a sour, stale smell lingers in the air.

At the back of the kitchen, a wooden door leads toward the manor gardens. Through the windows beside it, you can see overgrown hedges and flower beds choked with weeds. The garden looks close enough to touch, but the windows will not open and the back door will not move, both held shut by the same dark magic that protects the rest of the manor.

### 📍 The Dining Hall

You enter the dining hall and find a long table stretching beneath a heavy chandelier. Dust lies over the tableware, and the chairs remain pushed neatly into place as if the household might return to dinner at any moment. A meal has been left behind, but the food has decayed into a dark, unrecognizable mass beneath a layer of mold.

The portraits and polished woodwork suggest that this was once a room built for celebration. Now the only sound is the faint creak of the chandelier above you.

### 📍 The Living Room

You step into the living room and find beautiful furniture arranged around a broad fireplace. The carved wood, rich upholstery, and delicate decorations have survived the years better than anything in the town below, though a layer of dust dulls their former elegance. A piano rests in the corner, its closed lid coated in gray dust.

The fireplace is lit with a dark flame. It gives off no warmth, and its black light throws long shadows across the room without illuminating anything within them.

> 📜 Any attempt to put out the dark flame fails. Water, sand, magic, and physical interference pass through it or are consumed without changing its size or intensity. The piano appears to be intact, but none of the keys seem to function. The piano does not play any sounds because the same dark magic that silenced **Songbird Island** has silenced the piano.

### 📍 The Foyer

You enter the foyer beneath a grand chandelier that hangs from the high ceiling. A beautiful staircase curves upward toward the second floor, its banister polished despite the dust that covers the surrounding walls. Along the staircase wall hangs a collection of family paintings, showing a husband and his wife with their children at different ages.

The front doors stand at the far end of the foyer, but they are locked and protected by the same dark magic that sealed the wine-cellar door. The handles do not turn, and attempts to force the doors open leave no mark upon them.

### 📍 Bedroom (1): Cal's Bedroom

You enter the first bedroom. It is a simple room, with the bed, dresser, and nightstand filling most of the space. The former occupant left behind remnants of a life shaped by strength and honor: two battle axes mounted on the wall next to the dresser and a painting of a young man clad in armor with the town insignia on it.

> 📜 The crossed battle axes mounted on the wall cannot be removed, indicating that they may be protected by the same dark magic.

### 📍 Bedroom (2): Nevin's Bedroom

You enter the second bedroom and find a similar arrangement. The room has the same bed, dresser, and nightstand, but it also has a writing desk in the corner. Various books with titles relating to history and magic stack up on the desk, though they seem to be in poor condition. Above the dresser is a painting of a young man with a notebook in his hand. The remnants left behind paint a picture of a young scholar interested in history and the study of magic.

> 📜 Attempts to pick up and read any of the books cause them to crumble into dust in the party's hands.

### 📍 Bedroom (3): Ves' Bedroom

You enter the third bedroom, which is just as plain as the others. In place of a dresser is an armoire with a velvet purple dress inside it. A portrait of a young woman with a staff is mounted on the wall next to the armoire, but there does not seem to be any staff in sight around the room.

### 📍 Bedroom (4): Malek's Bedroom

You enter the bedroom at the end of the hall, and though it is a bit larger than the others, it is just as simple. There is a large bed in the center of the room with matching nightstands on both sides. On the far wall is a long dresser and next to it an armoire. Above the bed is a painting of a husband and wife with three young children standing in front of them.

### 📍 The Study

You enter the study across from the bedroom at the end of the hall. Shelving covers the walls from floor to ceiling, packed with books and ancient artifacts. The desk at the center of the room is buried beneath a mess of notes and loose papers, with pages spilling onto the floor. Among the papers, you find a journal. Its leather cover is worn and cracked from years of handling.

> 📜 Attempts to pick up and read any of the books from the shelves cause them to crumble into dust in the party's hands. The journal itself can be read and contains the entries detailing the descent into darkness that befell **Malek**.

#### 💬 The Descent Into Darkness

You pick up the leather journal left on the desk and open it. You skim through the journal, most of which is filled with passages of fun tales and adventures, but a few entries stand out. They seem to tell the tale of the descent into darkness that befell this scholar.

*"July 8th, 842 C.E.: I have returned from my trip visiting the shrine islands. They are truly fascinating. The glow from the orbs are so warm and pleasant. I have decided that my next adventure is to study the history of **Celestia**. I wasn't sure if her existence was true, but something tells me that she is real and she protects these lands. I have heard of a historian that resides in the town of **Oakwood** who may have more information. I shall seek him out."*

*"July 12th, 842 C.E.: I paid a visit to a man named **Jonah** who lives in **Oakwood**. He told me great tales about **Celestia** which seem like fairy tales, but he seemed so adamant that this was actual history and the tales were real. What really piqued my interest was that he talked about **Celestial Artifacts**. I've traveled the world and discovered a number of ancient artifacts, but I doubt that any of them belonged to **Celestia**. The story details her amulet being plunged into the depths of the oceans, posing an impossible feat to retrieve it. But he did also mention that she wore a golden ring with **Celestial** inscriptions etched into it."*

*"October 23rd, 842 C.E.: The world of **Thalassia** is vast, but it is mostly the sea with scattered islands about. I have visited all of the islands but one. It is the smallest of all the islands, so I did not suspect that it could have anything of importance. Furthermore, there was never any news of anyone traveling to or from this island. I intended to be the first one to travel to the White Sand Island."*

*"October 28th, 842 C.E.: I traveled to the White Sand Island and discovered that it actually has inhabitants. The entire island is empty except for a mother and her young children. Strange though, as the mother resembles nothing like the children and there is no father there. I attempted to talk to her about the island, about **Celestia** and even inquired where the children's father is, but she seemed to avoid these topics. She told me the father went off to sea and never returned, unsure of whether he left her and the children behind or died out at sea. She did not talk much about how she came to the island, simply claiming she grew up there herself. I remembered reading about how **Celestia** could take on human form in the books at the library at **Oakwood**. I suspect that the woman I talked to was actually **Celestia** in human form, but she was not wearing a ring. I suspect that the ring is hidden somewhere nearby on that island, but I spent days looking and could not find any hiding place. There is the chance that it simply resides in her family home, but I dare not break into someone's home."*

*"October 7th, 850 C.E.: It has been eight years since I first learned of the ring and amulet that belonged to **Celestia**. I feel that finding the ring is a fruitless endeavour, so I have spent the last year devising a plan to acquire the amulet at the bottom of the ocean. It may sound crazy, but I think I have a solution to this problem. I made a special request to the blacksmith to create a suit of armor that was both lightweight and completely sealed. This will be the suit that protects me from drowning. I also had the tailor weave in special fabrics which will allow the suit to have flexibility so I can actually function while wearing it. There remained two additional problems, the first was the ability to breathe long enough to reach the ocean depths. The second was in having a means to make it back to the surface safely. I had a local wizard enchant the suit to provide air within it which will allow me to breathe for an unnaturally long time within it and to give it immense strength to protect me from the ocean beasts. I had the same tailor that worked on the suit create a special rope made of strong silk fibers that stretches for about a mile. The plan is simply to use the suit to reach the depths of the ocean and use the rope to make it back to the surface once I find the amulet."*

*"May 2nd, 851 C.E.: The suit is complete and the rope is ready. I worked with the local fisherman to develop a layout of the ocean so that I could identify the possible location of the amulet. Tomorrow I head out. I have asked my children to assist me in this adventure along with my dear friend **Sir Roderick** from **Portsmith**. He is not a fisherman, but he is the person I trust the most with my life."*

*"May 4th, 851 C.E.: The adventure was a success! I have retrieved an amulet from the depths of the ocean. It almost cost me my life as the suit's magic began to fade. I feel that the amulet provided some sort of protection over me in that situation. I believe that this is the actual amulet of **Celestia**. I plan on adding it to my collection of artifacts that I have retrieved. Then again, the beauty of it deserves so much more than to sit on a shelf collecting dust. I may just decide to wear the amulet."*

*"May 5th, 851 C.E.: Not even a day has passed since I began wearing the amulet and I feel this strange sense of strength flowing within me. Is this the power of **Celestia**?"*

*"May 6th, 851 C.E.: I made my way through town to purchase some goods and I could not but help notice that the townsfolk around me seem so weak. They do not possess the strength that I now have. Even my own children seem frail and weak. I can't fix everyone, but I can fix my children."*

*"May 7th, 851 C.E.: Blood Red Moon. I found her, her sacrifice is necessary. **Malek** is no more. I am **Malekor**."*

## 💬 The Darkness Over Tidefall

After reading the journal entries, a voice speaks out: *"You shouldn't be here. You should have stayed away. You were warned."* You look around and do not see where the voice came from. A shadowy figure steps into the **Study**. It seems to be nothing more than a silhouette of a dark figure. *"You shouldn't be here. You should have stayed away. You were warned. My return from **The Abyss** is inevitable. Now you must die."*

### ⚔️ The Dark Void Shadow

👹 [Dark Void Shadow]

### 💬 The Vengeance Of Darkness

Upon the defeat of the **Dark Void Shadow**, the dark magic protecting the manor seems to fade away. The rooms all seem a bit brighter. You hear pounding at the door and head out to the top of the staircase to see what is happening. Suddenly, the front doors open and town guards pour in. They repeat in unison: *"Might I suggest visiting our **Shopping District** or our lovely **Town Square**."* They do not seem friendly, and they have their swords drawn. The blades are blood red with a black glow. As the guards make their way up the stairs toward you, you push the first one down the stairs, knocking down the rest of them, then run past them and through the front entrance. You head toward the front gate, now open, and step into the **Living Quarters**. A man steps out of his home and transforms into a **Shadow Hound**. A group of townsfolk walk toward you, each changing into a **Shadow Hound**. The guards catch up to you and begin to chase you. The numbers are too great; you must find a way to escape.

### 💬 Escaping Tidefall

You run toward the **Harbor**, hoping to make it off the island and escape the relentless darkness chasing at your heels.

> 📜 This is a short, fast-paced mini-game built around a **DC 13 (Dexterity / Acrobatics)** check for dodging and weaving, and a **DC 13 (Constitution / Endurance)** check for sustained running. Each party member makes both checks. These checks represent the party members running through the town of **Tidefall** with dark beasts and guards in pursuit. If a party member fails a check, the dark beasts and guards close the distance. Failing too many times causes the dark beasts and guards to attack the party members and inflict the **Mark of Darkness** upon them.
>
> * **Mark of Darkness:** Attacks do a flat 2-point reduction in damage, but cannot fall below zero damage.
>
> 💡 Use your best judgement to mix in both types of checks — dodging/weaving and endurance for running away quickly. Gauge how many checks each party member makes based on your judgement.

## 💬 The Harbor

You make your way to the **Harbor**, where you see a mob of dark beasts and guards trying to board **The Salty Sea-Dog**, while your companions keep them off the boat. **Captain Tobias** sees you running down the dock toward the ship and throws a rope toward you. *"Grab onto the rope and climb up!"*

You jump and grab the rope as **Captain Tobias** steers **The Salty Sea-Dog** away from **Tidefall**. **Bram** heads over to help pull you up.

**Captain Tobias** looks back at you, thankful that you made it aboard. *"I don't think we should ever return to **Tidefall**... it's clearly fallen to the darkness. The townsfolk and guards began attacking just a few minutes before you arrived, so I assume you must have found something important. Let me know where you want to head off to, and then get some rest — you look like you've been through hell and back."*

You look back and see **Tidefall** shrink in the distance. It is now clear that **Malekor** is the one who kidnapped your mother, because he believes her to be **Celestia** in human form. You ponder the possibility of that being true, and if so, you wonder whether she really did hide her ring, the **Ring of Celestia**, near your family home on **White Sand Island**.
