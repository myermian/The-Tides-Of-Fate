# Chapter 2.6: The Hidden Isle

## 💠 The Hidden Isle

> 📜 **The Hidden Isle** cannot be seen on ordinary maps. It can be found after the party has completed **Farro Island** and cleansed the corrupted shrines. The clues from Farro reveal that **Celestia** concealed important secrets, while the restored shrine magic makes the hidden island visible.

The island appears between waves where there was previously only open sea. It is small, gray, and silent. A ruined town covers most of the island, but every building has collapsed except for one stone church standing in the center.

The party can land directly beside the church. There are no surviving townsfolk and no required encounters in the ruins. The broken streets and empty homes show that the island was abandoned when the darkness reached it.

---

## 💬 The Ruined Town

The town is little more than foundations, fallen walls, and weeds growing through old stone. Faded symbols of **Celestia** appear on doorways and fragments of paving, all pointing toward the church.

The party can make a brief search of the ruins, but nothing here is necessary to enter the church. The important discovery is that this was once a place of worship and protection, not a settlement built by **Malekor**.

> 📜 The island should feel like the end of a trail. Keep the exploration focused and avoid adding side quests or unrelated treasure before the party reaches the church.

---

## ⛪ The Church Of Celestia

The church doors are weathered but unlocked. Inside, rows of stone benches face a simple altar beneath a circular opening in the roof. The walls are covered in faded murals showing **Celestia** bringing light to the sea and sealing a black opening beneath the world.

The murals establish three facts:

* The people of the island worshipped **Celestia**.
* She sealed a portal into the abyss beneath the church.
* The seal was intended to hold until someone bearing her ring could open it safely.

At the altar, a shallow circular recess matches the shape of **Celestia's ring**. Without the ring, the altar remains inert. With the ring, the recess fills with silver light and reveals a concealed door behind the altar.

> 📜 If the party has not found **Celestia's ring**, they can learn what lies beneath the church, but they cannot open the sealed passage. The ring is the story key that makes the final descent possible.

---

## 💬 The Descent

Behind the altar, a narrow stone staircase descends into the island. The air grows colder with every step, and the sound of the sea fades above. The walls change from church masonry to smooth black stone veined with pale celestial light.

The descent is direct. There are no branching paths or required puzzles. Along the walls, the party passes the remains of old protective symbols that have been cracked but not completely destroyed.

At the bottom, the stairs open into a vast underground chamber. A circular platform hangs above a dark chasm, connected to the shore by a narrow stone bridge. At the far end stands the sealed void portal.

---

## 🕳️ The Void Portal

The portal is a vertical wound in the air, surrounded by celestial runes. Blackness moves behind its surface as though something on the other side is watching the chamber.

The runes explain the purpose of the seal:

*"The darkness cannot be destroyed from this shore. Let the bearer of the ring cross the threshold and carry the light into the abyss."*

When a character wearing or holding **Celestia's ring** approaches, the runes respond. The portal opens, revealing a path into the abyss. The ring protects the party from being rejected by the seal and allows the entire party to pass through together.

Without the ring, the portal remains closed. The party can return to the surface and continue the campaign, but cannot enter the abyss yet.

> 💡 This is the final point of preparation. Allow the party to rest, change companions, and discuss their approach before entering. Once they cross the portal, they are entering the final encounter.

---

## ⚔️ The Abyss

The party emerges on a narrow platform suspended in endless darkness. Fragments of stone, broken weapons, and pieces of ancient architecture drift through the void. Far below, a dim red light pulses like a distant heartbeat.

At the center of the platform stands the **Dark Void Mage**. He wears robes that dissolve into smoke and holds a staff formed from black crystal. Behind him, the darkness gathers around a massive spell circle aimed at the world beyond the portal.

The mage turns toward the party as the portal closes behind them.

*"Celestia sealed this place and called it victory. Malekor opened the wound and called it power. I will finish what they began and give the void a world to inherit."*

### ⚔️ The Dark Void Mage (True Final Boss)

The **Dark Void Mage** is the true final enemy of the campaign. He draws power from the abyss and attempts to complete the spell that will spread eternal darkness across **Thalassia**.

The party must defeat him before the spell circle completes. The exact phases, abilities, and battle statistics are defined in the boss stat block.

## 💬 The End Of The Darkness

When the **Dark Void Mage** is defeated, the spell circle fractures and the abyss begins to collapse. The party sees a final burst of celestial light pass through the darkness and travel toward the distant world.

The portal reopens behind them, carrying the party safely back to the church beneath the Hidden Isle. The celestial runes seal the passage once more, but this time the seal is calm and whole.

Above ground, the ruined island changes. The clouds break, the sea becomes visible beyond the shore, and the first signs of life return among the old foundations. **The Hidden Isle** is no longer concealed, though it remains a quiet monument to **Celestia** and the people who protected the seal.

The party has defeated the true source of the darkness and saved **Thalassia**.
