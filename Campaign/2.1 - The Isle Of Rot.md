# Chapter 2.1: The Isle of Rot (Ignis & The Orb of Life)

## 🌴 Environmental Hazard: Corroding Mist

> 📜 This shrine contains the **Orb of Life**.
>
> While the **Orb of Life** is corrupted, at the start of every combat round, all players take **1d6 Necrotic Damage** and healing is only half as effective; healing by using Cora's Sea-Salve are not impacted by this.
>
> Cleansing the **Orb of Life** provides a universal blessing, `The Orb of Life Blessing`, which provides **1d6 health regeneration** to all party members at the beginning of each round.

---

## 💬 Arrival

The sea turns murkey green as you approach a jagged island of dying mangroves and black sludge. The smell of sulfer and decay makes your eyes water. **Captain Tobias** covers his mouth with a damp rag. *"Breath through your sleeves lads. This place is pure rot. I'm dropping you off at the rock ledge, then pulling back to open waters before this muck eats through my ship's hull. Signal me when the air clears and I'll pick you up."*

## Scene 2: Ignis’s Threat
**Ignis the Flesh-Rotter** stomps through the bile-covered clearing ahead, his rusted armor leaking black sludge:
> *"Pain... all there is is pain!"* Ignis roars, his voice wet and metallic. *"Father burned away my honor, and now I will burn away your skin! Feel the rot!"*

## !! BATTLE: Ignis the Flesh-Rotter (Boss Encounter)

| Stat | Value | Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Armor Class** | 19 (Rusted Abyssal Plate) | **Hit Points** | 260 |
| **Speed** | 30 ft | **Saving Throws** | STR +11, CON +11, WIS +5 |
| **Damage Immunities** | Necrotic, Poison | **Condition Immunities** | Poisoned, Exhaustion |

### Actions (DC 18 Save / +11 to Hit)
* **Multiattack:** Ignis makes three *Rot-Cleaver* attacks.
* **Rot-Cleaver:** Melee Weapon Attack: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 2d10 + 7 slashing damage + 2d6 necrotic damage. Target's maximum HP is reduced by the necrotic damage taken until a long rest.
* **Necrotic Eruption (Recharge 5–6):** Ignis slams his greataxe into the ground. All creatures within 20 ft must make a **DC 18 CON Save** or take **8d6 necrotic damage** and be **Poisoned** for 1 minute (Half damage, no condition on success).

### Legendary Actions (3 per Round)
* **1 Action - Heavy Stomp:** Ignis slams his foot down. Target within 5 ft must succeed on a **DC 18 STR Save** or be knocked **Prone**.
* **2 Actions - Sludge Jet:** Ignis fires a line of acidic sludge (30 ft line, 5 ft wide). **DC 18 DEX Save** or take 4d6 acid damage.
* **3 Actions - Boiling Rage:** Ignis recharges *Necrotic Eruption* immediately.

## Cleansing the Shrine
Defeating Ignis purifies the land. Touching the orb grants the **Orb of Life Blessing** (*All party members regenerate 1d8 + 4 HP at the start of combat turns*). Tobias returns to pick up the party.
