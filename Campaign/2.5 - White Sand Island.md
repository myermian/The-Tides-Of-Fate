# Chapter 2.5: White Sand Island

## 💠 White Sand Island

> 📜 **White Sand Island** is a quiet island of white beaches, rolling hills, and a family farm overlooking the sea. This is the home island of the party.
>
> 💡 The island should feel safe and familiar when the party first visits it. There is also a hidden cave at the base of the hill which cannot be discovered until the party has visited **Farro Island** and learned about the **Ring of Celestia**.

## 💬 Arrival

The water turns a clear light blue as **The Salty Sea-Dog** approaches the island. Beyond the shallows are the white beaches at the base of the rolling hills. The island looks peaceful and untouched.

**Captain Tobias** slows the ship and studies the water ahead. After a moment, he shakes his head. *"The water's too shallow for **The Salty Sea-Dog** to reach that dock. A smaller ship could make it, but mine's too big for these waters. I'll take you as close as I can, then you can wade the rest of the way yourself. This isn't one of the shrine islands, that much is for sure. Still, if you've got reason to come here, I'm content to wait offshore until you've found what you are looking for."*

The ship anchors just beyond the shallows, close enough that the water is only a short distance from the beach. You climb down from **The Salty Sea-Dog** and wade through the calm, shallow water toward the white sand shores. The walk is easy, and you soon stand on the beach of your home. Looking around, it seems that the darkness spreading throughout **Thalassia** has not reached your home quite yet.

> 📜 The party can return to **The Salty Sea-Dog** at any time they are ready to leave.
>
> 💡 No other character knows that **White Sand Island** is the party's home unless the party told that character during an earlier conversation. If this information is revealed, use your best judgement to determine which other characters know and adjust narration and interactions as appropriately.

---

## 🗺️ Exploration

The party can explore the island freely. The locations are close enough to reach on foot, and each one can provide a moment of rest, memory, or quiet investigation.

* **The White Shores**
* **The Rolling Hills**
* **The Farm House**

### 📍 The White Shores

You make your way to the **White Shores**, named for the beautiful white sand forming the beach. A small wooden dock extends into the clear, shallow water, where your ship once docked before it was destroyed during your initial rescue efforts. Off in the distance, you see **The Salty Sea-Dog** anchored, with **Captain Tobias** watching over you from aboard the ship.

### 📍 The Farm House

You make your way up to your family farm, sitting atop the hill above the beach. It is a humble but familiar home surrounded by fields, old footpaths, and the remains of your childhood memories. The door was left open in your haste to rescue your mother, and it sways gently in the wind.

#### 💬 The Family Home

You enter the home through the open front door. The first floor contains the kitchen and dining area, as well as the family room where you spent countless hours listening to your mother tell you stories. Upstairs, you find the bedrooms, each one still holding the quiet traces of the life you left behind. At the end of the upstairs hallway is your mother's room. It is a simple room with a bed and a nightstand and nothing else.

> 📜 The number of bedrooms should match the size of the party. Give each character a private room or sleeping space, and adjust the layout as needed to fit the party's history. Their mother's room is intentionally empty, including the nightstand; it contains none of her personal belongings. The simplicity of the room and absence of personal items feels strange, especially in a home otherwise filled with traces of your childhood, which foreshadows that her personal items were hidden somewhere else, nearby.
>
> 💡 There is nothing important to discover in the home beyond memories and opportunities for roleplay. A familiar object, room, or story may remind the party of **Elora**, but do not reveal that she is **Celestia** here.

### 📍 The Rolling Hills

You make your way to the rolling hills east of your family farm. The hills are covered in pale grass and wildflowers and stretch across the island towards the eastern waters. You make your way to the top of the tallest peak. From here, you can see the entire island. This place evokes memories of you running around during your childhood. Beyond that, there doesn't seem to be anything else here.

> 📜 If the party already knows that **Celestia** is tied to **White Sand Island**, they can search the area for anything that seems strange. Their search reveals subtle clues pointing to a cave at the base of the hills and gives them the opportunity to discover it. Without this prior knowledge, the party finds nothing beyond the ordinary landscape and childhood memories.

---

## 💬 The Hidden Cave

With the knowledge that **Celestia** is tied to this island, you recognize that the clues point toward what appears to be a solid wall at the base of the hills. When you walk toward it, you pass through the surface and enter a hidden cave. The cave is a small, makeshift room illuminated by soft **Celestial Light**. Paintings cover the walls in a mural-like fashion, depicting **Celestia** and the party as children. At the far end of the room, a simple table holds a mysterious box.

### The Mysterious Box

You open the mysterious box and discover a golden ring with **Celestial** inscriptions carved into it. The chamber fills with a warm presence. You have discovered the **Ring of Celestia**.

> 📜 The **Ring of Celestia** is a unique item that grants protection from the darkness to the entire party. It also allows the party to enter **The Abyss** through the portal beneath **The Hidden Isle**. The party can then confront **The Dark Void Mage** for the true ending.
>
> * **Ring of Celestia:** *(Wondrous Item, Unique)* A golden ring with **Celestial** inscriptions on it. The ring provides a flat 2-point damage reduction to the entire party from each hit.
