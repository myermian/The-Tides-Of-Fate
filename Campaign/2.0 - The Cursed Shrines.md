# Chapter 2: The Cursed Shrines

## Chapter Overview

> 📜 The campaign is now far more open for exploration and unique game mechanics come into play. In addition to the the three islands with the shrines, there are more places to visit in the world of **Thalassia**; the party is free to choose their next destination in any order they wish.
>
> There are three shrine islands which have been corrupted by the dark void. Upon visiting a shrine island, **Captain Tobias** and the other companions stay behind and the party is unable to leave the shrine island until the chapter is completed. Cleansed shrine islands can be revisited and are completey safe (no environmental hazards, no combat).
> * Localized Penalty: Shrine islands will have a localized penalty applied against the party which varies based on the island.
> * Universal Buff: After the orb is cleansed and the chapter is concluded, the island becomes completely safe and eminates a glow across all of **Thalassia**, providing a universal buff for the remainder of the campaign. The party signals to **Captain Tobias** that they are ready to return to the ship and they leave the island.
>
> The party cannot visit Portsmith until all of the shrines have been cleansed. If the party attempts to do so, **The Salty Sea-Dog** will run into the tempest and **Captain Tobias** will refuse to head into it to avoid the ship from being destroyed.
>
> 💡 Revisiting **Oakwood** provides no additional content or benefits. If the players choose to do so, provide dialogue to indicate that they should continue on their campaign.
>
> 💡 The additional islands in **Thalassia** are not required, but visiting them provides additional content for the campaign. The party can choose to leave these islands if no other conditions prevent them from doing so prior to completing that sub chapter.
> 
> Full Rest: When traveling to an island, the party is provided with the benefits of a full rest.
> [Companions]: The crew that joined you on **The Salty Sea-Dog** are busy keeping the ship operating, but the party can choose one campanion prior to visiting an island to gain additional benefits while on that island. The benefits expire are temporary and expire after leaving the island.
>
