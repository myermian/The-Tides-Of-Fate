# Chapter 2: The Cursed Shrines

## 💠 Chapter Overview

You board onto the **The Salty Sea-Dog**. **Ryan** approaches you, *"Here, let me help you out with your gear."* He takes your gear and leads you down below. *"It's a bit tight in here, but these are your beds. It's a bit of a journey between islands, so you will get your fill of rest."* **Cora**, sitting on her bed, chimes in *"Don't worry. We will take care of manning the ship so you guys can be at full strength."* **Ryan** begins to introduce you to your companions. *"I'm not sure who you've met, but let me introduce you to everyone."*

* *"This here is **Bram**. He can help strengthen your gear and armor."*
* *"You already **Jonah**, this here is his son, **Gideon**. He's just as smart and can tell you everything you need to know about the beasts and hazards of an island."*
* *"Over here is our lovely **Lyra**, the most beautiful voice in all of **Oakwood**. If you are ever feeling down, she'll get your spirits up."*
* *"This young boy is **Finnegan**. He's studying to become a wizard. Can you believe it... 14 years old and he's already mastered the elements."*
* *"This is **Finn**, not to be confused with **Finnegan**. He is very stealthy, so he can scout the island ahead and give you the lay of the land. Quite the artist with the details."*
* *"Of course, you know **Cora**, her salves healed you. If you need her to, she can brew up some additional salves, but they have a short shelf-life."*
* *"And lastly, if you need someone to keep you on your toes, I'll be happy to be your sparring partner and keep you sharp."*

**Captain Tobias** makes his way down the stairs and approaches everyone. *"I want to thank you all for volunteering to help out."* He turns, looks at **Ryan**, *"Son, I know we don't always get along. I've always wished you went into fishing like your old man, but I'm proud of you. As a matter of fact, I'm proud of all of you that stepped up to help. We only needed six of you and we got seven volunteers."* He turns to you, *"You all are taking the heavy burden of this journey, we'll man this ship, you just rest up. And, given that we only need six to keep this ship going, figure out who mans the ship and who gets the special assignment of aiding you. Once you figure it out, let me know where you want to head to. I'll be at the helm."* **Captain Tobias** heads up stairs, the rest of the crew stands at the ready waiting to know who mans the ship and who gets the special assignment of assisting you.

> 📜 The campaign is now far more open for exploration. In addition to the the three shrine islands, there are more places to visit in the world of **Thalassia**; the party is free to choose their next destination in any order they wish.
>
> The campaign now introduces additional game mechanics:
> * Full Rest: When traveling between islands, the party is provided with the benefits of a full rest.
> * [Companions]: The crew that joined you on **The Salty Sea-Dog** are busy keeping the ship operating, but the party can choose one companion prior to visiting an island to gain additional benefits. The benefits are temporary and expire after leaving the island. You are free to select different companions between different island visits.
> * Corrupted Orb: Shrine islands will have a localized penalty applied against the party members which varies based on the island.
> * Universal Blessing: After the orb of a shrine is cleansed, the island becomes completely safe and the orb emits a glow across all of **Thalassia**, providing a universal blessing for the remainder of the campaign.
>
> There are three shrine islands which have been corrupted by the darkness. Upon visiting a shrine island, **Captain Tobias** and the other companions stay behind on **The Salty Sea-Dog**. The party is unable to leave the island until the island is cleansed. Attempting to revisit a cleansed shrine island will lead to **Captain Tobias** informing the party that time is of the essence and they should continue onto the next shrine island.
>
> The party cannot visit the island of Portsmith due to the tempest until all of the shrines have been cleansed. **Captain Tobias** will refuse to head there, reminding the party that **Jonah** said that the shrines need be cleansed first. After the shrine islands are cleansed, the party is free to head to Portsmith.
>
> The additional islands in **Thalassia** are not required, but visiting them provides additional content for the campaign. The party can choose to leave these islands if no other conditions prevent them from doing so prior to completing that sub chapter.
>
> 💡 If the players choose to revisit **Oakwood**, decide on whether **Captain Tobias** agrees, or keeps the party on task. This is an opportunity to introduce additional content.
