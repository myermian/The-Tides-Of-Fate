# Chapter 2.2: The Isle of Silence (Nil & The Orb of Power)

## 🌴 Environmental Hazard: Dark Silence

> 📜 This shrine contains the **Orb of Power**.
>
> While the **Orb of Power** is corrupted, the island has a void aura covering it, preventing high-tier arcane energy. This limits spells and upcasting to a maximum of Level 4. Additionally, all spells have a double cost.
>
> Cleansing the **Orb of Power** provides a universal blessing, `The Orb of Power Blessing` which provides a free upcast to all spells.
