# Chapter 2: The Corrupted Shrines

## 💠 Chapter Overview

You board **The Salty Sea-Dog** and **Ryan** approaches you. *"Here, let me help you out with your gear."* He takes your gear and leads you below deck. *"It's a bit tight in here, but these are your beds. It's quite a journey between islands, so you will get your fill of rest."* **Cora**, sitting on her bed, chimes in. *"Don't worry. We will take care of manning the ship so you can be at full strength."* **Ryan** begins to introduce you to your companions. *"I'm not sure who you've met, but let me introduce you to everyone."*

* *"This here is **Bram**. He can help strengthen your gear and armor."*
* *"You already know **Jonah**. This is his son, **Gideon**. He's just as smart and can tell you everything you need to know about the beasts and hazards of an island from historical records."*
* *"Over here is our lovely **Lyra**, the most beautiful voice in all of **Oakwood**. If you are ever feeling down, she'll get your spirits up."*
* *"This young boy is **Finnegan**. He's studying to become a sorcerer. Can you believe it... 14 years old and he's already mastered the elements."*
* *"This is **Finn**, not to be confused with **Finnegan**. He isn't much of a fighter, but he is very stealthy, so he can scout the island ahead and give you the lay of the land."*
* *"Of course, you know **Cora**. Her salves healed you, so if you need her to, she can brew up some additional salves, but they have a short shelf-life."*
* *"And lastly, if you need someone to keep you on your toes, I'll be happy to be your sparring partner and keep you sharp and ready."*

**Captain Tobias** makes his way down the stairs and approaches everyone. *"I want to thank you all for volunteering to help out."* He turns and looks at **Ryan**. *"Son, I know we don't always get along. I've always wished you went into fishing like your old man, but I'm proud of you. As a matter of fact, I'm proud of all of you who stepped up to help. We only needed six of you, and we got seven volunteers."* He turns to you. *"You are taking on the heavy burden of this journey. We'll man this ship while you rest up. Given that we only need six to keep this ship going, figure out who mans the ship and who gets the special assignment of aiding you. Once you figure it out, let me know where you want to head. I'll be at the helm."* **Captain Tobias** heads up to the ship's deck. The rest of the crew stands ready, waiting to learn who will man the ship and who will receive the special assignment of assisting you.

> 📜 The campaign has now entered into an open world experience.
> 
> 🗺️ The party is free to explore the world of **Thalassia** with some restrictions:
>
> 📍 There are three shrine islands which have been corrupted by the darkness. Upon visiting a shrine island, **Captain Tobias** and the other companions stay behind on **The Salty Sea-Dog**. The party is unable to leave the island until the shrine is cleansed. Attempting to revisit a cleansed shrine island will lead to **Captain Tobias** informing the party that time is of the essence and they should continue onto the next shrine island.
>
> 📍 The island of **Portsmith** is where **Malekor** is residing. This is only accessible after all three shrine islands have been cleansed; attempting to visit **Portsmith** early will result in **Captain Tobias** warning that the tempest is too strong, suggesting that they follow the advice of **Jonah** and cleanse the shrines first. After the shrines are cleansed, the party is free to head to **Portsmith**.
>
> 📍 The other islands are not required to complete the campaign, but instead offer additional content and a true ending.
>
> 📖 The campaign now introduces additional game mechanics:
> * Long Rest: When traveling between islands, the party automatically gains the benefits of a **Long Rest**.
> * [Companions]: The crew that joined you on **The Salty Sea-Dog** are busy keeping the ship operating, but the party can choose one companion prior to visiting an island to gain additional benefits. The benefits are temporary and expire after leaving the island. You are free to select a different companion on each island visit.
> * Corrupted Orb: Shrine islands impose a localized penalty on the party, which varies by island.
> * Celestial Blessing: After the orb is cleansed, the shrine island becomes completely safe, and the orb emits a glow across all of **Thalassia**, providing a **Celestial Blessing** for the remainder of the campaign.
>
> 💡 If the players choose to revisit **Oakwood**, decide on whether **Captain Tobias** agrees or keeps the party on task. This is an opportunity for the Dungeon Master to introduce additional content.
