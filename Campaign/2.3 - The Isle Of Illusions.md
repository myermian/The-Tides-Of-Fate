# Chapter 2.3: The Isle of Illusions (Vespera & The Orb of Clarity)

## 🌴 Environmental Hazard: Illusionary Bends

> 📜 This shrine contains the **Orb of Clarity**.
>
> While the **Orb of Clarity** is corrupted, the island has is shrouded in illusions causing all creatures (party) to have a disadvantage roll on all perception checks.
>
> Cleansing the **Orb of Clarity** provides a universal blessing, `The Orb of Clarity Blessing` which provides all creatures (party) to have an advantage roll on all perception checks.
