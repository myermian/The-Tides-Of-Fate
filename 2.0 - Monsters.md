## Chapter 1: The Ash of Oakwood

### 🐉 Gloomgura *(Large Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil)*

Reference: [Barlgura](https://5e.tools/bestiary.html#barlgura_xmm)

A barlgura twisted by the void's darkness, the **Gloomgura** crawls out from the **Dark Forest** where it feeds on the living. Its hide has blackened to a tar-like sheen, and its eyes burn with a cold, hollow light. Where a barlgura serves the Abyss, the Gloomgura serves only the spreading dark, lashing out with a tormenting bite that sears the mind as surely as the body.

☠️ **Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP; PB +3)

* ✨ **Demonic Restoration:** If the Gloomgura dies outside the Abyss, its body dissolves into ichor, and it gains a new body instantly, reviving with all its Hit Points somewhere in the Abyss.
* 🗡️ **Multiattack:** The Gloomgura makes one Tormenting Bite attack and two Thrash attacks.
* 🗡️ **Tormenting Bite:** Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 11 (2d6 + 4) Piercing damage plus 13 (2d12) Psychic damage.
* 🗡️ **Spellcasting:** The Gloomgura casts one of the following spells, requiring no Material components and using Wisdom as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 13): 2/day each — Disguise Self, Invisibility (self only); 1/day each — Entangle, Phantasmal Killer (level 6 version).
* 🗡️ **Thrash:** Melee Attack Roll: +7, reach 5 ft. Hit: 9 (1d10 + 4) Bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it has the Prone condition.
* ➕ **Leap:** The Gloomgura jumps up to 40 feet by spending 10 feet of movement.
