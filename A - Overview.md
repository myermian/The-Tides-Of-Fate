# D&D: The Tides of Fate

## The Legend of Malakor & The Red Moon Ritual

Lord Malakor was not born evil. Once a renowned scholar, his ruin began with his obsession of lost treasures. Deep within a sunken trench near his home island, he dredged up the Amulet of the Abyss—an ancient artifact housing a dark, dormant entity.

Upon touching the amulet, its cursed power surged into Malakor, corrupting his mind with delusions of absolute dominion. Desiring supreme rule over the entire archipelago, he used the amulet's dark magic to corrupt his own three children—Vespera, Ignis, and Null—stripping away their humanity and twisting them into his faithful lieutenants. He dispatched them to the three surrounding island shrines, where they infected the ancient protective Orbs that once shielded the realm from darkness.

With the Orbs infected and the realm defenseless, Malakor set his ultimate plan into motion. He discovered the true identity of **Elora**—an angelic goddess living in human form, raising orphaned children on a hidden farm. Malakor kidnapped her to his mountain stronghold for one horrific purpose: to sacrifice her under a celestial alignment, transforming the sky's full moon into a blood-red moon. This blood sacrifice will tear open a permanent portal to the Abyss, allowing dark entities to swallow the entire ocean realm whole.

## Campaign Rules & Mechanics (DM Quick Reference)

* **Party Size/Scaling:** Scalable for 3 to 6 Players (Level 11).
  * **Hard Mode**: A full party of 6 players is strongly recommended for Hard Mode.
  * **Small Party**: If the party consists of only 3 players, Ryan Earth-Binder joins the party as a permanent NPC companion to balance action economy and frontline defense.
* **Resting & Logistics:** Boarding *The Salty Sea-Dog* automatically grants a **Long Rest** and fully restores spell slots, health, and provisions.
* **Healing Provision:**
  * **Apothecary Salves** (Bonus Action to use; restores minor health). Restocked by Captain Tobias on every voyage (Default: 3 per player per island). The healing is **3d10 + 4 HP** (easy), **3d8 + 4 HP** (normal), and **3d6 + 4 HP** (hard).
  * **Potions of Full Healing**: (Full Action) Completely restores HP to maximum. Provided by Captain Tobias before the final assault on Portsmith.
* **Archipelago Orb Blessings (Stacking Global Buffs):**
  * **Orb of Life (Isle of Rot):** Clears *Corroding Mist*. All party members automatically **regenerate 1d8 + 4 HP** at the start of their combat turns.
  * **Orb of Power (Isle of Silence):** Clears *Mana Suppression*. 
    * *Spellcasters (Any class):* Gain **+1 extra spell slot per level (1st–6th)** and all spells are upcasted 1 level for free.
    * *Martial/Non-Caster Classes:* Gain **+1 extra Action Surge or Resource Use** (e.g., Ki Points, Rage) per rest and **+2 damage** on all weapon attacks.
  * **Orb of Clarity (Isle of Fog):** Clears *Fog of Doubt*. Grants permanent **Advantage** on the party's first attack roll or saving throw each turn.

### Companion NPC Stat Block: Ryan Earth-Binder

**Ryan Earth-Binder** *Medium Humanoid (Human), Neutral Good*

| :-------------- | :-------------------------- | :-------------------- | :-------------------- |
| **Armor Class** | 18 (Plate Armor & Shield)   | **Hit Points**        | 115 (11d10 + 44)      |
| **Speed**       | 30 ft.                      | **Saving Throws**     | STR +8, CON +8        |

| **Languages**   | Common                      | **Challenge / Level** | Level 11 Companion    |

**Traits**
- **Protection Style**: When a creature Ryan can see attacks a target other than him that is within 5 feet of him, Ryan can use his Reaction to impose Disadvantage on the attack roll.
- **Sentinel**: When Ryan hits a creature with an Opportunity Attack, the creature's speed becomes 0 for the remainder of the turn.

**Actions**
- **Multiattack**: Ryan makes two weapon attacks with his Longsword.
- **Longsword**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 1d8 + 4 slashing damage (or 1d10 + 4 if used two-handed).
- **Shield Bash (Bonus Action)**: Ryan attempts to shove a creature within 5 feet of him using his shield. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked Prone.

**Tactical AI & DM Guide for Ryan**
- **Primary Role**: Frontline Defender / Tank.
- **Combat Behavior**: Ryan positions himself between vulnerable party members and the primary melee threats. He prioritizes protecting low-HP casters or squishy party members using his **Protection Style** reaction.
- **Action Priority**:
  1. Use **Shield Bash** as a Bonus Action to knock dangerous foes Prone, giving melee allies Advantage on attacks.
  2. Attack the nearest priority target using **Multiattack**.
  3. Use **Protection Style** on his Reaction whenever an adjacent ally is targeted by a melee or ranged attack.
- **Out-Of-Combat Utility**: Ryan assists with heavy lifting, door breaches, and physical checks, stepping back to let players take the lead during diplomatic encounters.

## Characters

### Dark Lord Malakor (Formerly Malek)
Before his dark descent, Malek was a respected, visionary scholar, renowned for his fierce love for his family. Everything changed when his obsession with ocean antiquities led him to the Abyssal Amulet. The artifact’s sentient malice warped his noble drive into ruthless tyranny, twisting his name to Lord Malakor and consuming his soul with visions of world-spanning conquest. Now, he is a cold, imposing warlord clad in tide-carved armor, devoid of mercy or remorse. Where he once sought honor and adventure, he now sees people—even his own blood—as mere instruments to sacrifice under the Red Moon to usher in eternal darkness.

### Vespera The Mind-Weaver (Formerly Ves)
Once a bright, empathetic young scholar named Ves, she was her father’s pride, known for her sharp intellect and gentle nature. When Malakor corrupted his family, he flooded her brilliant mind with the dark psychic whisperings of the Abyss, breaking her spirit and twisting her empathy into psychological cruelty. Reborn as Vespera the Mind-Weaver, she has become a haunting, levitating figure wrapped in dark silks whose voice echoes directly inside her victims' heads. She takes sadistic pleasure in unraveling the minds of her enemies, using illusions, mental fog, and psychic screams to force others to suffer the same torturous madness that broke her own mind.

### Ignis The Flesh-Rotter (Formerly Ignan)
Before the corruption, Ignan was Malakor’s eldest son—a protective, golden-hearted warrior who trained diligently to defend his home island and shield his younger siblings. Malakor used the dark amulet to burn away Ignan’s honor, replacing his warmth with an insatiable, agony-fueled necrotic rot. Now known as Ignis the Flesh-Rotter, he is a towering, armored brute encased in rusted plate mail that perpetually leaks black, acidic sludge. Incapable of feeling anything but searing physical pain and rage, Ignis charges into battle as a hulking force of decay, seeking to inflict his constant, burning misery onto everyone around him.

### Null The Spell-Binder (Formerly Nevin)
The youngest of the three siblings, Nevin was a quiet, inquisitive boy fascinated by the flow of natural ley-lines and ancient arcane structures. During Malakor’s dark ritual, the amulet's void magic forcefully severed Nevin’s connection to the world’s natural magic, hollowing out his personality and leaving him an unfeeling, stoic husk. Reborn as Null the Spell-Binder, he is a cold, calculated anti-mage warrior who speaks in flat, emotionless tones. He wields a massive greatsword capable of cleaving through spells, driven solely by a ruthless directive to extinguish all magic that displeases his father.

### Elora (Angelic Goddess)
Elora (true name Celestia) is a warm, graceful woman with subtle silver flecks in her eyes and golden-tinged hair. Even in rags aboard Malakor's ship, she radiates a gentle, soothing warmth. She is devoted, protective, and serene. She came across a group of young children who lost their mother and father to an illness. She decided to hide her divinity and raise them as a normal mother on an uninhabited island farm. Malakor discovered her true divine nature through his dark amulet. He needs her divine blood to sacrifice under the full moon to turn it into the Red Moon and open the Abyssal portal.

### Captain Tobias
Captain Tobias is a weathered sailor in his late 50s with a braided gray beard, deep sea-wrinkles, and a peg leg made of treated ironwood. He is gruff, pragmatically optimistic, and immensely loyal to his home. He speaks in steady, nautical turns of phrase and loves a good sea shanty. He finds the party adrift out at sea and rescues them, bringing them to his fishing village, Oakwood. He uses his vessel, *The Salty Sea-Dog*, to ferry them across the archipelago, keeping them supplied with provisions and ensuring they get enough rest between trips to be fully ready for battle.

### Elder Miriam
Elder Miriam is a tall, matriarchal woman in her late 60s wearing draped woven shawls and carrying a walking staff topped with smoothed driftwood. She is stern, wise, and cautious, but deeply burdened by the suffering of her people under the orb's corruption. When Captain Tobias brings the party to Oakwood, she asks them for help to fend off the beasts attacking their village.

### Cora Earth-Binder
Cora is a young woman with ink-stained fingers, messy copper hair, and leather pouches brimming with dried sea-herbs tucked into her apron. She is quirky, fast-talking, and obsessed with brewing remedies from strange flora. She treats the party's injuries after their rescue. She crafts the Apothecary Salves (**healing**, Bonus Action) that Captain Tobias restocks for the party throughout their voyage.

### Bram Iron-Hand
Bram is a broad, burly dwarf with a singed leather apron, thick arm tattoos depicting sea-serpents, and a soot-stained face. He is plain-spoken, hard-working, and fiercely appreciative of martial talent. When Captain Tobias brings the party to Oakwood, Bram repairs their equipment and armor, continuing to offer maintenance throughout their journey.

### Ryan
Ryan is Captain Tobias’s son and the father of Kaelen and Lily. Unlike his father, who dedicated his life to the sea, Ryan chose the path of a shield-bearer, training rigorously in martial combat to defend Oakwood from landward threats. He is a steadfast, quiet man in his early 30s with broad shoulders, his father's determined jawline, and well-kept steel armor emblazoned with the village crest. Ryan holds deep respect for his father's seamanship and works closely with Bram Iron-Hand to keep Oakwood's defenses secure. While he usually stays behind to protect his children and the village elders, he is a formidable warrior ready to step up and fight alongside visiting heroes when extra muscle or protection is needed.

### Kaelen
Kaelen is the older of Captain Tobias's grandchildren. At age 10, he hopes to join his grandfather as a fisherman one day. For now, he lives on the island spending his days learning about the sea and playing with his younger sister.

### Lily
Lily is the youngest of Captain Tobias's grandchildren. At age 6, she spends her days in the fields picking flowers and playing with her older brother.

### Old Man Jonah
Jonah is the oldest person in Oakwood and former keeper of the local library. He spends his days teaching young children various skills and remembers what life was like before Malakor infected the realm with his darkness.

### Maeve
Maeve is the innkeeper of Oakwood (*The Salty Drift*). She cooks and provides local delicacies to visitors and locals, sourcing her goods from her farm on the outskirts of town.

### Sir Roderick The Unyielding
Sir Roderick is a former high-ranking knight of Portsmith and Lord Malek’s closest companion. Overcome by terror when Malek was corrupted by the dark amulet, Roderick went into hiding within Malek's abandoned manor while the town fell. Burdened by guilt and shame, he aids the party by revealing Malek's past and—if visited by the heroes—finds his redemption by crashing into the final battle to shatter the cursed amulet.
