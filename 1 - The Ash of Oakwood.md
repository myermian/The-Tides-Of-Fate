# Chapter 1: The Ash of Oakwood

## Scene 1: Waking Up at The Salty Drift

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> You open your eyes to unfamiliar wooden rafters. The air smells of dried salt, sea-kelp, and faint chamomile. You are lying in soft linen beds inside a warm, rustic room. 
>
> Before you can fully gather your bearings, the door clicks open. A young woman with messy copper hair, ink-stained fingers, and a leather apron loaded with dried herbs steps inside. Seeing you sit up, her eyes light up with excited relief.
>
> "Oh! You're awake! Praise the tides, I wasn't sure my poultices would hold against that ocean rot!"

### Roleplaying Cora Earth-Binder
* **Cora's Explanation:** She introduces herself as the local apothecary. When asked where they are, she explains that **Captain Tobias** dragged them out of the ocean while riding out a storm on his fishing boat. He brought them straight to **The Salty Drift** (the village inn) so she could tend to their injuries.
* **The Salves:** She hands each player a pouch containing **3x Apothecary Salves** (*Bonus Action to use; restores health*). She beams with pride, telling them to keep them handy before heading out to check on other townspeople.

---

## Scene 2: The Main Hall & Lay of the Land

When the players head downstairs into the inn's common room, they are greeted by **Maeve**, the innkeeper.

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> Downstairs, a hearty woman wiping down an oak table gives you a warm, welcoming smile. A plate of freshly baked bread, roasted roots, and pan-seared fish is set down before you.
>
> "Eat up, heroes," she says warmly. "You've been through hell. I'm Maeve, owner of this fine establishment."

### Roleplaying Maeve
* **Local Info:** Maeve explains she grows most of her ingredients on her family farm on the outskirts of town.
* **Directions:** She tells the party that **Elder Miriam** is expecting them at the **Town Hall**, but encourages them to look around Oakwood first:
  * **Cora’s Apothecary:** (*Currently empty; Cora is out gathering herbs.*)
  * **The Docks:** Where Captain Tobias and his son, Ryan, tend to the boats.
  * **The Blacksmith Forge:** Run by Bram Iron-Hand.
  * **The Library / Square:** Where Old Man Jonah teaches the children.
* If asked further questions, Maeve excuses herself, noting she has to get back to prepping the evening stew, and suggests talking to Old Man Jonah if they want local history.

---

## Optional Scene 3: Exploring Oakwood

Players are free to explore the village before going to the Town Hall.

### A. The Docks (Captain Tobias & Ryan)
* **Interaction:** Tobias is mending nets with his son, **Ryan**. 
* **Dialogue:** Tobias laughs heartily, amazed they survived: *"Normally I don't venture out that far when a tempest brews, but something pulled my eye to the water. Glad you're among the living!"*
* **Ryan:** Introduced as a quiet, heavily armored defender of the village.
* **Push:** Tobias insists they go see Elder Miriam right away.

### B. The Blacksmith Forge (Bram Iron-Hand)
* **Interaction:** Bram is hammering iron over an anvil.
* **Dialogue:** *"Ah, the drowned rats are walking!"* Bram chuckles. He tells the party he personally repaired their weapons and armor after the shipwreck.
* **Location of Gear:** He notes that he delivered their gear straight to the **Town Hall**, so they'll need to see Elder Miriam to collect it.

### C. The Town Square / Library (Old Man Jonah, Kaelen, & Lily)
* **Interaction:** Old Man Jonah is sitting on a bench teaching **Kaelen (10)** and **Lily (6)** how to tie naval knots.
* **Children:** Kaelen boasts about wanting to be a grand fisherman like his grandfather Tobias; Lily happily shows off a handful of wildflowers.
* **Lore Drop (Malakor):** If asked about the realm, Jonah sighs: *"It wasn't always dark skies and monster tides. Lord Malakor—or Malek, as we once called him—was a noble captain. But the deep sea holds things that should stay buried. Once he brought up that obsidian amulet, darkness swallowed his heart."*

### D. Leaving Town (The Guard Post)
* If players try to leave Oakwood, town guards politely block the road: *"Hold on there, travelers. The woods and roads are crawling with aggressive beasts lately. Elder Miriam is waiting for you at the Town Hall—speak with her first."*

---

## Scene 4: The Town Hall & Elder Miriam's Plea

Inside the Town Hall, **Elder Miriam** sits behind a long wooden table, flanked by the party's cleaned armor and weapons.

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> Elder Miriam looks up with stern, tired eyes. "Welcome, travelers. I am glad to see you breathing. Bram has restored your equipment, and you are welcome to rest in Oakwood for as long as you need. But tell me... what brought you formidable warriors into the heart of Malakor's storm?"

### The Conversation
1. **The Party’s Story:** The players explain their quest to rescue their mother, Elora, from Lord Malakor.
2. **Miriam’s Request:** Hearing of their martial prowess, Miriam’s eyes brighten with desperate hope. She explains that Oakwood has been plagued by an influx of corrupted sea-monsters. Specifically, **Maeve’s farm on the outskirts** is currently under siege. She asks if they would lend their strength to help defend the village.

---

### Branching Path A: The Party Agrees to Help
* Miriam thanks them profusely and points them toward Maeve’s farm.
* **Battle Location:** Maeve's Farm.
* **NPCs:** Villagers cheer for their bravery.

### Branching Path B: The Party Declines to Help
* **Miriam’s Response:** Disappointed, but understanding: *"I see. You have your own burdens. We shall handle our own troubles as best we can."*
* **Narrative Consequence:** The monsters break through the farm undefended and march straight into the village square.
* **Villager Reactions:** As the fight begins in the town square, villagers remark coldly: *"We pulled you from the sea and fed you... the least you could do is help us!"*
* **Battle Location:** Oakwood Town Square.

---

## Scene 5: The Kidnapping

Immediately after the raid is repelled, **Old Man Jonah** stumbles into view, out of breath and terrified.

> "The woods! A giant shadow-troll burst from the treeline during the chaos—it grabbed Kaelen and Lily and dragged them off into the dark forest!"

Captain Tobias and Ryan rush forward, pleading with the heroes to help rescue the children.

---

### Branching Choice: Rescuing the Children

#### Option 1: The Party Agrees Immediately
* The heroes give chase right away into the Whispering Woods.

#### Option 2: The Party Declines
* **Tobias:** Mutters *"Cowards..."* under his breath.
* **Ryan:** *"We're going without you!"* Ryan grabs his sword and shield, and he and Tobias sprint into the forest alone.
* **Miriam's Second Plea:** Elder Miriam steps forward, begging: *"Ryan is a fine guardian, but he cannot face a troll alone, and Tobias is an old fisherman! Please, I beg you, reconsider!"*

##### Sub-Choice 2A: Party Declines a Second Time (GAME OVER)
* The party stays at the inn. That night, a massive horde of monsters emerges from the forest, completely overwhelming Oakwood. Without the children saved or the forest cleared, the village falls, and the party is killed in the collapse. **Campaign Ends.**

##### Sub-Choice 2B: Party Agrees After Miriam’s Second Plea
* The party chases into the forest. Halfway there, they find **Ryan and Captain Tobias lying battered and incapacitated** on the trail.
* **Dialogue:** Ryan coughs, pointing ahead: *"It... took them further up... please... save my children..."*

---

## Scene 7: The Forest Clearing (Mini-Boss)

The party reaches a cavernous clearing where the kidnapped children, **Kaelen and Lily**, are trapped in a suspended wooden cage. Guarding them is a towering **Corrupted Mire-Troll**.

---

## !! BATTLE: The Corrupted Mire-Troll (Mini-Boss)

### Boss Stat Block: Corrupted Mire-Troll

| Stat | Value | Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Armor Class** | 15 (Mire-Crusted Hide) | **Hit Points** | 110 |
| **Speed** | 30 ft | **Saving Throws** | STR +7, CON +6, WIS +0 |
| **Damage Vulnerabilities** | Acid, Fire | **Condition Immunities** | Frightened |

#### Actions & Abilities
* **Multiattack:** The Troll makes three attacks: one with its *Bite* and two with its *Claws*.
* **Bite:** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 1d8 + 4 piercing damage.
* **Claws:** +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 2d6 + 4 slashing damage.
* **Corrupted Roar (Recharge 5–6):** The troll lets out an abyssal shriek. All players within 20 ft must succeed on a **DC 14 WIS Save** or be **Frightened** for 1 minute (repeat save at end of turn).

#### Special Traits
* **Abyssal Regeneration:** The troll regains **10 HP** at the start of its turn. If the troll takes **Fire or Acid damage**, this trait doesn't function at the start of the troll's next turn.

## Scene 8: Resolution & Setting Sail

With the Mire-Troll slain, the heroes free Kaelen and Lily from the cage and guide them safely back to Oakwood.

### Returning to Town

> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> As you emerge from the forest treeline carrying Kaelen and Lily, the entire village of Oakwood erupts into tears and cheers. 

* **If Party Agreed Immediately:** Tobias and Ryan wrap the children in tight embraces, praising the heroes as true legends.
* **If Party Agreed Late:** Ryan and Tobias (having been patched up by Cora back in town) limp over, holding their kids tight. Tobias remarks: *"I was wrong about you... you came through when it mattered most."*

### Captain Tobias's Pledge
Seeing their valor and hearing of their divine quest to rescue their mother, Captain Tobias steps forward with absolute resolve:

> "You saved my grandchildren and brought light back to Oakwood. As long as blood pumps through my veins, *The Salty Sea-Dog* is yours. I'll ferry you to the ends of the earth to break Malakor's hold and bring your mother home."

DM NOTE (Small Party Check):
- If Party Size is 3: Ryan Earth-Binder steps forward, gripping his longsword and shield with determination. "You saved my children when I was too weak to protect them," Ryan says, his voice thick with emotion. "I won't let you face Malakor's forces alone. I'm coming with you." (Ryan joins the party as an active combat companion; see stat block in `A - Overview.md`).
- If Party Size is 4 or more: Ryan grips the heroes' hands in deep gratitude. "I must stay behind to keep Oakwood's defenses secure while you are away. May the tides favor you, heroes."

### Chapter Ending

Captain Tobias instructs the party to get a good night's sleep at *The Salty Drift*. He promises to spend the night stocking his ship with fresh water, rations, and Cora’s **Apothecary Salves**.

The heroes take a well-deserved **Long Rest**. Tomorrow, they set sail on their adventure, but which shrine do they visit first?
