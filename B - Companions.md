# The Tides of Fate: Companions

Before disembarking **The Salty Sea-Dog** at a new island or location, the party can consult with **one companion** to gain their shipside utility buff.

In addition to their main utility buff, each companion grants all party members a bonus to two specific Saving Throws based on the campaign difficulty:
* **Easy:** +3 Bonus
* **Normal:** +2 Bonus
* **Hard:** +1 Bonus

---

## Cora (Apothecary)
* **Class / Archetype:** Master Apothecary (Cleric / Rogue)
* **Utility Buff — Sea-Salve Stash:** Provides the party with field salves that restore **1d10 HP** per use. 
  * **Easy:** 3 Salves
  * **Normal:** 2 Salves
  * **Hard:** 1 Salve
* **Saving Throw Boost:** Grants a bonus to **Wisdom** and **Charisma** Saving Throws.
* **Expiration:** Unpreserved organic sea-botanicals spoil and lose their potency once exposed to sea air, causing any unused salves to break down upon returning to the ship.

---

## Bram (Iron-Hand)
* **Class / Archetype:** Master Blacksmith (Artificer)
* **Utility Buff — Tempered Gear:** Magic-tempers equipment, granting a flat bonus to both **Attack Rolls** and **Armor Class (AC)**.
  * **Easy:** +3 Attack Rolls & +3 AC
  * **Normal:** +2 Attack Rolls & +2 AC
  * **Hard:** +1 Attack Roll & +1 AC
* **Saving Throw Boost:** Grants a bonus to **Strength** and **Constitution** Saving Throws.
* **Expiration:** The specialized sea-steel oils and temporary rune-etchings wear off under the friction of battle and time, returning gear to its baseline stats during your next Long Rest.

---

## Ryan (Earth-Binder)
* **Class / Archetype:** Vanguard Shield-Bearer (Fighter)
* **Utility Buff — Vanguard Fortitude:** Coordinates defensive drills before departure, granting bonus **Maximum Hit Points** to all party members until the next Long Rest.
  * **Easy:** +3d10 Max HP
  * **Normal:** +2d10 Max HP
  * **Hard:** +1d10 Max HP
* **Saving Throw Boost:** Grants a bonus to **Strength** and **Constitution** Saving Throws.
* **Expiration:** The physical conditioning, extra padding, and tactical momentum naturally wear off as the party cools down and rests aboard the ship.

---

## Gideon (Scholar)
* **Class / Archetype:** Tactical Archivist (Inquisitive Rogue / Wizard)
* **Utility Buff — Tactical Dossier:** Briefs the party on local monster lore across all difficulties, revealing enemy stat blocks (HP, AC, Save DCs, and Weaknesses) and identifying hidden environmental hazards.
* **Saving Throw Boost:** Grants a bonus to **Intelligence** and **Wisdom** Saving Throws.
* **Expiration:** Gideon's research applies strictly to the local fauna and terrain of the specific island mapped; the notes become obsolete upon setting sail for a new location.

---

## Lyra (Sea Siren)
* **Class / Archetype:** Sea-Singer (Bard)
* **Utility Buff — Sailor's Shanty:** Performs ancient maritime shanties, granting every party member a **Bardic Inspiration** die before disembarking.
  * **Easy:** 1d10 Bardic Inspiration
  * **Normal:** 1d8 Bardic Inspiration
  * **Hard:** 1d6 Bardic Inspiration
* **Saving Throw Boost:** Grants a bonus to **Charisma** and **Dexterity** Saving Throws.
* **Expiration:** The magical resonance and uplifting tempo of her sea shanty fade naturally from the mind when the party sleeps and rests aboard the vessel.

---

## Finnegan (Wizard)
* **Class / Archetype:** Tide-Shaper (Druid / Sorcerer)
* **Utility Buff — Oceanic Ward:** Channels protective sea magic, granting resistance to elemental damage types (Fire, Cold, and Lightning).
  * **Easy:** Resistance to 3 Elements (Fire, Cold, and Lightning)
  * **Normal:** Resistance to 2 Elements (Party Choice)
  * **Hard:** Resistance to 1 Element (Party Choice)
* **Saving Throw Boost:** Grants a bonus to **Intelligence** and **Constitution** Saving Throws.
* **Expiration:** The protective tide-veil woven around the landing party naturally dissipates as its magic weaves back into the surrounding sea during a Long Rest.

---

## Finn (Scout)
* **Class / Archetype:** Coastal Infiltrator (Rogue)
* **Utility Buff — Infiltration Routes:** Maps out hidden paths and sentry blind spots, granting the party a **Surprise Attack Round** on the first round of all non-boss combat encounters across all difficulties.
* **Saving Throw Boost:** Grants a bonus to **Dexterity** and **Intelligence** Saving Throws.
* **Expiration:** Mapped coastal routes, choke points, and patrol blind spots apply strictly to the specific shoreline explored and provide no tactical benefit at sea or on other islands.
