# Chapter 2: The Shattered Shrines

## Chapter Overview & DM Notes
* **Sandbox Structure:** The party can visit the three corrupted islands in **any order**. Each section below serves as a self-contained island module.
* **Recommended Progression:** The islands are ordered by difficulty below (Easiest $\rightarrow$ Hardest):
  1. **2.1 — The Isle of Rot** (Ignis / Brute Force)
  2. **2.2 — The Isle of Silence** (Null / Mana Suppression)
  3. **2.3 — The Isle of Fog** (Vespera / Heavy Crowd Control & Illusions)
* **Captain Tobias Dialogue & Journey Tracker:**
  * **Before Island 1:** Captain Tobias asks, *"Where to first?"* If asked for advice, Tobias responds: *"If you're asking me, start with the **Isle of Rot**. It's nasty, but straight forward brute force. Save the **Isle of Silence** for next, and leave the **Isle of Fog** for last—that mind-mist is pure malice."*
  * **Before Island 2:** Captain Tobias asks, *"Where to next?"*
  * **Before Island 3:** Captain Tobias says, *"One more to go, I'm all stocked up, rest up, I'll wake you when we get there."*
* **Logistics & Isolation Rule:** Upon dropping the party off at any island, **Captain Tobias** declares he is returning to Oakwood (or staying off-coast) to restock supplies and prepare the ship. He will return at dawn or when signaled. This prevents early exits or retreats mid-dungeon.
* **Resting Rule:** Boarding *The Salty Sea-Dog* between island trips automatically grants a **Long Rest** and fully restocks **Apothecary Salves** (3 per player).

## 2.1 — The Isle of Rot (Ignis & The Orb of Life)

### Environmental Hazard: Corroding Mist
Until the **Orb of Life** is cleansed, necrotic acid pollutes the air and tidepools. At the start of every combat round, all players take **1d6 Necrotic Damage** and cannot regain HP through natural means (non-magical healing/salves still work).

### Scene 1: Arrival & Tobias’s Departure
> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> The sea turns murky green as you approach a jagged island of dying mangroves and black sludge. The smell of sulfur and decay makes your eyes water.
>
> Captain Tobias covers his mouth with a damp rag. "Breathe through your sleeves, lads. This place is pure rot. I'm dropping you at the rock ledge, then pulling back to open waters before this muck eats through my ship's hull timbers. Signal me when the air clears!"

### Scene 2: Ignis’s Threat
**Ignis the Flesh-Rotter** stomps through the bile-covered clearing ahead, his rusted armor leaking black sludge:
> *"Pain... all there is is pain!"* Ignis roars, his voice wet and metallic. *"Father burned away my honor, and now I will burn away your skin! Feel the rot!"*

#### Actions (DC 18 Save / +11 to Hit)
* **Multiattack:** Ignis makes three *Rot-Cleaver* attacks.

* **3 Actions - Boiling Rage:** Ignis recharges *Necrotic Eruption* immediately.

### Cleansing the Shrine
Defeating Ignis purifies the land. Touching the orb grants the **Orb of Life Blessing** (*All party members regenerate 1d8 + 4 HP at the start of combat turns*). Tobias returns to pick up the party.

## 2.2 — The Isle of Silence (Null & The Orb of Power)

### Environmental Hazard: Mana Suppression
Until the **Orb of Power** is cleansed, the island’s void aura chokes high-tier arcane energy.
* **Spell Limit:** Spells of **4th level or higher cannot be cast**, and lower-level spells **cannot be upcasted past 3rd level** (attempts to do so automatically fail and waste the action, but do not consume the spell slot).
* **Martial Burden:** Martial features cost double resource points (e.g., 2 Ki points or 2 Superiority Dice instead of 1).

### Scene 1: Arrival & Tobias’s Departure
> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> The Isle of Silence rises like an obsidian tomb from the ocean. No birds fly here; no wind blows through the dead spires. An unnerving, dead silence presses against your ears.
>
> Captain Tobias points to the black stone docks. "I can't hear the sound of my own oar-strokes here," he whispers nervously. "I'll be waiting a mile offshore out of range of this dead-zone. Ring the shrine's bell when you're done, and I'll row back in."

### Scene 2: Null’s Challenge
**Null the Spell-Binder** stands atop the steps of the silent cathedral, wielding a broad void-blade:
> *"Magic is a flaw,"* Null speaks in a flat, hollow tone. *"Father stripped away my connection to the ley-lines. Now, I will strip away yours."*

### !! BATTLE: The Void Vanguard

* **Enemies:** 2x Void-Bound Myrmidons & 2x Spell-Eater Sentinels.

### !! BATTLE: Null the Spell-Binder (Boss Encounter)

| Stat | Value | Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Armor Class** | 20 (Void-Forged Armor) | **Hit Points** | 240 |
| **Speed** | 35 ft | **Saving Throws** | STR +10, DEX +8, CON +9, INT +9 |
| **Damage Immunities** | Force | **Condition Immunities** | Stunned, Silenced |

#### Actions (DC 18 Save / +10 to Hit)
* **Multiattack:** Null makes three *Mage-Slayer Greatsword* attacks.
* **Mage-Slayer Greatsword:** Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 2d8 + 6 slashing + 2d8 force damage. If the target is a spellcaster, they lose their highest remaining spell slot up to 3rd level.
* **Nullification Nova (Recharge 5–6):** Null releases a pulse of anti-magic in a 30-ft radius. All ongoing spells/buffs in the area are dispelled (**Dispel Magic DC 18**), and spellcasters must make a **DC 18 CON Save** or take **6d10 force damage**.

#### Legendary Actions (3 per Round)
* **1 Action - Void Step:** Teleports up to 30 ft to an unoccupied space adjacent to a spellcaster.
* **2 Actions - Spell Sunder:** Makes one *Mage-Slayer Greatsword* attack.
* **3 Actions - Void Aura (1 Round):** Emits a 20-ft aura of total silence until his next turn. No spells with Verbal components can be cast within it.

### Cleansing the Shrine
Defeating Null restores the ley-lines. Touching the orb grants the **Orb of Power Blessing** (*Casters get +1 spell slot per level 1st–6th; Martials get +1 Action Surge/Resource & +2 damage*). Tobias returns to pick up the party.

## 2.3 — The Isle of Fog (Vespera & The Orb of Clarity)

### Environmental Hazard: Fog of Doubt
Until the **Orb of Clarity** is cleansed, a dense, psychic fog blankets the island. All creatures have **Disadvantage on Perception checks** and **Ranged Attack Rolls** beyond 30 feet.

### Scene 1: Arrival & Tobias’s Departure
> *Read or paraphrase to the players:*
>
> The heavy bow of *The Salty Sea-Dog* cuts through water as thick as gray soup. A blanket of unnatural fog hugs the shoreline, swallowing the sun. 
>
> Captain Tobias grips the helm, squinting into the gloom. "This ain't natural sea-fog," he mutters. "Smells like old copper and bitter thoughts. Keep your minds guarded."
>
> He lowers the rowboat. Once your feet hit the cold sand, Tobias nods firmly. "I'll head back toward Oakwood to restock on Cora's salves and fresh water. I'll be back at dawn to pick you up. Don't let the mist play tricks on your heads!" 
>
> With a push of his oar, *The Salty Sea-Dog* fades back into the mist, leaving you on the silent shore.

### Scene 2: Vespera’s Taunts
As the party advances, **Vespera’s psychic projection** appears in the swirling fog:
> *"So... The Goddess' stray children come to play hero,"* her voice echoes inside your minds. *"Did you really think you could piece together what the Dark Lord broke? Look at yourselves—desperate, lost, clinging to memories of a mother who belongs to the Abyss now."*

### !! BATTLE: The Fog-Bound Vanguard

* **Enemies:** 2x Mist-Horror Behemoths & 3x Mind-Gouger Specters.

### !! BATTLE: Vespera the Mind-Weaver (Boss Encounter)

| Stat | Value | Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Armor Class** | 18 (Abyssal Barrier) | **Hit Points** | 225 |
| **Speed** | 10 ft, Fly 40 ft (Hover) | **Saving Throws** | INT +10, WIS +9, CHA +8 |

#### Actions & Spells (DC 18 Save / +10 to Hit)
* **Multiattack:** Vespera uses *Psychic Blast* twice.
* **Psychic Blast:** Ranged Spell Attack: +10 to hit, 90 ft. *Hit:* 4d8 + 6 psychic damage.
* **Scream of Agony (Recharge 5–6):** 30-ft cone. **DC 18 WIS Save** or take **8d8 psychic damage** and be **Stunned** until end of next turn (Half damage, no stun on success).
* **Mirror Mirage (1/Day):** Creates 3 illusory duplicates (AC 15).

#### Legendary Actions (3 per Round)
* **1 Action - Mind Dart:** Makes one *Psychic Blast* attack.
* **2 Actions - Mist Shift:** Teleports up to 30 feet in the fog without provoking opportunity attacks.
* **3 Actions - Phantasmal Terror:** Target within 60 ft makes a **DC 18 WIS Save** or takes 4d10 psychic damage and is **Frightened** for 1 minute.

### Cleansing the Shrine
Defeating Vespera clears the fog. Touching the orb grants the **Orb of Clarity Blessing** (*Advantage on first attack roll or saving throw each turn*). Tobias returns to pick up the party.

## Scene 3: The Convergence & Chapter Outro

> *Read after the third and final orb is cleansed and the party has boarded the ship:*
>
> With the third dark orb purified, Captain Tobias pulls the rowboat alongside *The Salty Sea-Dog*. You climb aboard the deck, exhausted but triumphant, as the crew casts off into open water.
>
> Suddenly, the ocean violently swells and the ship rocks beneath your feet. 
>
> Looking back across the water, three massive pillars of light erupt into the night sky from the cleansed island shrines: a brilliant **green beam** from the Isle of Rot, a glowing **red beam** from the Isle of Silence, and a radiant **blue beam** from the Isle of Fog. The three beams pierce through the swirling tempest clouds above, meeting at a single point high in the atmosphere.
>
> A blinding **beam of pure white light** cascades straight down through the shattered storm clouds, striking the mountain peak of Dark Lord Malakor’s island stronghold. The impenetrable Abyssal Barrier surrounding his lair shatters like glass, dissolving into shimmering mist across the sea.
>
> Captain Tobias grips the wheel, staring in awe at the newly exposed fortress on the horizon.
>
> "I hope you are ready," Tobias says quietly, turning to face you with grim resolve. "Malakor will be quite a challenge. Rest up. I'll let you know when we arrive."
