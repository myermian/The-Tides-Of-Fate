## Final Campaign Boss

### Dark Lord Malakor
*The ruler of shadow and abyssal magic, empowered by the cursed amulet.*

| Stat | Value | Stat | Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Armor Class** | 20 (Abyssal Plate) | **Hit Points** | 320 |
| **Speed** | 40 ft, Fly 30 ft | **Saving Throws** | STR +12, CON +11, WIS +8, CHA +10 |
| **Damage Resistances** | Cold, Fire, Lightning | **Damage Immunities** | Necrotic, Poison |
| **Condition Immunities**| Charmed, Frightened, Prone, Stunned | **Senses** | Truesight 120 ft |

#### Actions (DC 19 Spell Save / +12 to Hit)
* **Multiattack:** Makes three attacks with his *Abyssal Greatsword* or uses *Void Obliteration* once and makes one weapon attack.
* **Abyssal Greatsword:** Melee Weapon Attack: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit:* 2d10 + 7 slashing damage + 2d8 necrotic damage.
* **Void Obliteration (Recharge 5–6):** 60-ft line (10 ft wide). Each creature in the line must make a **DC 19 DEX Saving Throw**, taking **10d6 force damage** on a failure, or half on a success.
* **Drain Life (1/Day):** Target within 30 ft must make a **DC 19 CON Save** or take **6d10 necrotic damage**, healing Malakor for the amount of damage dealt.

#### Legendary Actions (3 per Round)
* **1 Action - Teleport Slash:** Teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space next to a target and makes one *Abyssal Greatsword* attack.
* **2 Actions - Dark Pulse:** All creatures within 15 ft must succeed on a **DC 19 STR Save** or take 3d6 necrotic damage and be pushed 20 ft away.
* **3 Actions - Shadow Channel:** Recharges *Void Obliteration* immediately.
